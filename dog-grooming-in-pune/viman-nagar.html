Frequently asked questions

Do you offer early morning slots for airport-area residents?

Yes. Early slots are available and popular in Viman Nagar. Confirm the time window when booking.

How do you keep a white-coated dog actually white after bathing?

We use a whitening shampoo designed for light coats and follow with a thorough blow-dry that lifts the coat and prevents yellowing. We also check for staining around the muzzle and paws.

Is grooming recommended year-round or just in summer?

Year-round. Different seasons bring different coat and skin needs — summer deshedding, monsoon skin hygiene, post-winter coat conditioning. Consistent grooming maintains health through all of them.

Can you maintain a specific trim style my dog has always had?

Yes. Tell us the style or show us a reference photo when booking. Our groomers follow briefs precisely.

Is there an age minimum for grooming?

We groom puppies from around 10–12 weeks onwards, once their vaccination schedule is sufficiently advanced. Early grooming builds positive associations for life.

How do I prepare my dog for the session?

A short walk or toilet break before the groomer arrives is ideal. A slightly calmer dog cooperates better. No need to bathe beforehand — that's our job.

What is the least painful slot to book given the Phoenix and Nagar Road traffic?

Mid-morning, roughly 10 AM to 12:30 PM, or mid-afternoon. The stretch past the mall between 6 and 9 PM is where arrival windows slip most, and if you want an evening session we would rather book you at 7 PM with an honest twenty-minute window than promise a time we cannot hold.

We are relocating abroad. What does my dog need done before the flight?

A full bath two to three days ahead, so the coat is completely dry before crate time, plus short nails and trimmed paw hair — both matter more for crate comfort than a fresh haircut does. Book the vet health certificate tightly to the flight date; book the groom loosely. A bath on travel morning leaves a damp undercoat in a cold hold.

Our society will not allow drying or grooming on the balcony. Does that restrict the session?

Not at all — the whole session happens indoors as standard. We would not air-dry on a balcony in any case: Pune humidity keeps an air-dried coat damp at the skin for hours, and that is how fungal patches begin. The bath uses your bathroom and the dry and trim use any clear indoor floor.

Can you groom two pets of different sizes in one booking?

Yes. Tell us both breeds when you book so the slot is realistic — a Lab and a Shih Tzu together is close to a double session, not one and a half. We groom them one at a time and ask that the waiting pet stays in another room, because a dog watching another dog being dried usually gets more agitated, not less.

Is there a lift and parking constraint for groomers in the high-rises here?

Nothing that affects you. Our groomers arrive by two-wheeler with the entire kit in one shoulder bag, so parking pressure around Viman Nagar towers is not a factor and any lift, including a service lift, is sufficient. If your tower entrance is hard to find from the main gate, a pin saves the groomer a call.
