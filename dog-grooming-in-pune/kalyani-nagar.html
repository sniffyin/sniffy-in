Frequently asked questions

Is at-home grooming genuinely better than a salon for urban dogs?

For most dogs, yes. The absence of other animals, unknown smells, and waiting areas reduces anxiety significantly. In a dense urban area with traffic, removing the commute alone improves the experience for both dog and owner.

What space do you need inside the flat to set up?

A bathroom or balcony with water access is ideal. A space roughly the size of a large bathroom is sufficient. We manage our own equipment and keep everything contained.

Do you check for ticks during the session?

Yes, always. A thorough tick and skin check is part of every session. If we find anything concerning, we tell you immediately.

How long does a full session take for a medium-sized dog?

Typically 60 to 90 minutes. Larger breeds or dogs requiring detangling may take slightly longer. We never rush.

Can I schedule at a specific time to fit my work-from-home schedule?

Yes. We offer morning, afternoon, and evening slots. You confirm the window that works and we arrive within that timeframe.

Do you provide a grooming report after the session?

Yes. At the end we give you a brief verbal rundown of what we observed — coat condition, skin, ears, nails — and what to watch for before the next session.

Our building insists service visitors use the service lift. Is that an issue?

None at all — the groomer follows whatever your building requires and carries everything in one bag. It is worth telling us if the service lift is on a different tower entrance, because that is usually where a groomer loses ten minutes finding their way in a large Kalyani Nagar complex.

Can the dog be dried on the balcony? Our society has rules about that.

The blow-dry happens indoors as standard, so society balcony rules rarely come into it. We would not air-dry a dog on a balcony anyway — Pune humidity means a coat left to air-dry stays damp at the skin for hours, and damp skin under a thick coat is how fungal patches start. Every session ends with a proper blow-dry to the skin indoors.

Do you have experience with imported breeds that are uncommon in Pune?

Yes, and Kalyani Nagar is where we see most of them. Coats we handle regularly here include Bichons, Cockers, Shih Tzus, Huskies, Samoyeds and Standard Poodles. If your dog is a breed with an established clip we would rather work from a photograph of how it has been cut before than from a breed-standard name, which different groomers interpret differently.

We are relocating overseas. What grooming should be done before the flight?

Nails short, paw hair trimmed, sanitary area clean, and a full bath finished two to three days ahead so the coat is genuinely dry before the crate. Do not book a bath for travel day. If the destination requires a vet health certificate, the vet visit — not the groom — is the thing to schedule tightly around the flight date.

I am often not home. Can my house help be present for the session instead?

Yes, as long as someone the dog is comfortable with is there and you are reachable by phone. What we will not do is take a decision that changes your dog — clipping down a matted coat, for instance — on a staff member's say-so. That call comes to you directly, even if it means the groomer waits on the line.
