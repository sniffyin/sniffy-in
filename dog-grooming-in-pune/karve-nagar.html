Frequently asked questions

How do you handle very old or senior dogs during grooming?

We slow the entire session down — more breaks, gentler restraint, monitoring the dog's energy levels throughout. We also avoid prolonged standing and use non-slip surfaces so older dogs feel secure.

Do you send the same groomer for repeat bookings?

We do this wherever possible. Groomer consistency reduces anxiety for dogs who remember familiar people. In Karve Nagar especially, this has made a visible difference in our repeat clients' dogs.

What if my dog has health conditions that affect grooming?

Please mention any health conditions when booking — arthritis, skin conditions, ear problems, anxiety. We adjust our approach accordingly and always work within what the dog is comfortable with.

Do you groom both dogs and cats?

Yes. Cat grooming is available in Karve Nagar, including for long-haired domestic cats and Persians.

Are your shampoos suitable for dogs with skin sensitivities?

Yes. We carry mild, fragrance-free, and hypoallergenic options for dogs with documented sensitivities. Mention this when booking and we'll prepare accordingly.

How do I know the session is going well if I'm not watching?

We brief you fully at the end — what we observed, what we did, and anything to watch for. Your dog's demeanour after the session is often the best indicator: a calm, relaxed dog means the session went well.

Do you cover Dahanukar Colony, Erandwane and the Anand Nagar side?

Yes, along with the Warje and Shivane end of Karve Nagar. It is a dense, well-established area and easy for us to reach, which usually means we can hold tighter arrival windows here than in the outlying parts of the city.

Water pressure in our older building is weak. Is that a problem for bathing?

Not really. The groomer works from a bucket for the lather and rinse stages anyway, so a thin tap is slower to fill but changes nothing about the wash itself. If your supply is timed rather than just weak, keep three or four buckets ready before the slot and the session runs exactly as it would otherwise.

How does booking work during Ganpati week around here?

Book well ahead — a week at minimum. The lanes off Karve Road and Paud Road become genuinely unpredictable during processions and we cannot hold arrival windows the way we normally would. For a noise-sensitive dog, the more useful plan is to groom in the quiet week before the festival rather than during it.

Our grandchildren are usually at home. Is it a problem if kids are around during the session?

Only if the dog is not used to it, and in a house where children are always present that is rarely the case. What genuinely helps is keeping children out of the room during the dryer and clipper stages — a dog managing a loud tool plus excited kids has two things to cope with, and the clipper stage is the one where a startled movement matters most.

Can I book a nail trim on its own between full sessions?

Yes. A short maintenance visit for nails, ear cleaning and a paw-hair trim is available below package price — ask for it by name. For a senior or short-coated dog it is often all that is genuinely needed between full grooms, and it saves putting an older dog through a full bath more often than the coat requires.
