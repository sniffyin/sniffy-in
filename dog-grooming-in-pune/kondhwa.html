Frequently asked questions

Is your grooming service suitable for gated residential communities?

Yes. Our compact setup and structured scheduling make it ideal for organized residential complexes.

Do you handle long-haired breeds carefully?

Yes. Long coats require gentle brushing, detangling, and moisture balance. We follow a careful process to avoid discomfort.

What if my pet has minor matting?

We assess matting severity and recommend safe detangling or trimming methods to prevent skin injury.

Are skin-friendly shampoos used?

Yes. Products are selected based on coat type and sensitivity to ensure safe application.

Can I stay present during grooming?

Yes, you are welcome to observe the session while allowing the groomer adequate working space.

How often should grooming be scheduled for small breeds?

Small and long-haired breeds usually benefit from grooming every 3–5 weeks.

Do you cover the NIBM Road side and the Undri end as well as Kondhwa Khurd and Budruk?

Yes, all of it — Kondhwa Khurd, Kondhwa Budruk, the NIBM Road corridor and out toward Undri and Mohammed Wadi. It is a spread-out area with several societies sharing similar names, so a location pin at booking saves the groomer a phone call from your gate.

Can you groom a large breed inside a flat, or does it need outdoor space?

A flat is fine. A large dog needs room to stand and turn, not a lawn — the bathroom for the wash and any clear floor for the dry and trim is enough. What genuinely helps with a big double-coated dog in an apartment is doing the deshedding stage on a balcony if you have one, simply because it contains the volume of loose undercoat.

Power cuts are common in our area. What happens if the electricity goes mid-session?

The dryer is the only part that needs power, so a cut pauses the session rather than ending it. If your building has an inverter, tell the groomer which sockets are backed up — that is usually enough to keep the dryer running. A dog left part-dried is the one outcome we will not accept, so if power is genuinely out we towel-dry thoroughly and wait.

Do your groomers speak Marathi and Hindi?

Yes. Our groomers work in Marathi, Hindi and functional English, and Kondhwa is a mixed enough area that all three come up in a normal week. If an elderly family member will be handling the visit rather than you, tell us and we will make sure the groomer and the household have a shared language for the instructions that matter.

Is it harder to get a booking around festivals and long weekends?

Yes, noticeably. Eid, Diwali and long weekends are when everyone wants the dog looking presentable on the same two days, and those slots go about a week in advance across Kondhwa and NIBM. If your dog is on a regular cycle, booking the next session before the groomer leaves is the simplest way to avoid the crush.
