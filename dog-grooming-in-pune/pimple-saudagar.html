Pune seasons

Monsoon and Dust: the Two Things This Area Does to a Coat

Construction dust in summer, ticks from June onwards.

Pimple Saudagar is still building, and the dust that comes with it settles into coats faster than most owners expect — particularly in short dense coats where it sits against the skin rather than on top. Through summer that means deep cleansing baths and more frequent brushing than the breed strictly requires.

From June the problem inverts. Warm, wet months are when the Indian brown dog tick breeds fastest, and a dog walked on the green patches around Rahatani and Sai Chowk comes back with passengers. Every monsoon session here includes a tick and flea check, a full ear check, and a complete blow-dry down to the skin — the drying is the medically useful part, not a finishing flourish.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover Rahatani, Pimple Gurav and Jagtap Dairy as well as Pimple Saudagar proper?

Yes, all of them, plus Pimple Nilakh, Thergaon and the Sangvi side — at the same published prices. The area names blur into each other here, so if your society is known locally by the builder name rather than the locality, send a pin and we will confirm the slot.

Will a full grooming session leave hair all over my flat?

No. The groomer works on his own mats in a contained area, collects hair as it comes off rather than at the end, and sweeps and wipes the space before leaving. This is the most common worry we hear in this area and it is the one people are most surprised about afterwards.

The parking here is impossible. Where does the groomer park?

He does not need to. Our groomers arrive by two-wheeler with the entire kit — clippers, dryer, shampoos, towels, mats, disposables — in a bag. There is no van, no generator, and nothing that needs a visitor parking bay or a society NOC.

Can you do an evening or weekend slot?

Yes — we run 9 AM to 9 PM seven days a week. Weekday evenings and weekend mornings are the busiest windows in Pimple Saudagar, so book a few days ahead for those rather than same-day.

My Spitz sheds constantly. Should I just get her shaved for summer?

No, and we will decline if you ask. A double coat insulates against heat as much as cold, and a shaved one regrows patchy and coarse — often permanently. The correct answer is deshedding: an undercoat rake plus a high-velocity blow-out that removes the dead coat while leaving the guard hairs doing their job.

How often should my dog be groomed here?

It depends on the coat, not the calendar. Long non-shedding coats like Shih Tzus need every 4 to 6 weeks, double coats every 6 to 8 with deshedding, short dense coats like Beagles and Pugs every 8 to 12, and Indies usually every 8 to 10. Construction dust in this area pushes many families slightly more frequent than the minimum.

Do you do tick and flea treatment during monsoon?

Yes — it is a ₹149 add-on and we recommend it from June through September. Warm wet months are when the Indian brown dog tick breeds fastest, and dogs walked on the green patches around Rahatani and Sai Chowk pick them up readily. Every monsoon session also includes a full ear check and a complete blow-dry to the skin.

Can you groom two dogs in the same visit?

Yes. Each dog is priced as its own session, and the visit simply runs longer — usually a little under double a single-dog booking rather than exactly double. Tell us both breeds at booking so the groomer plans the order, because it is rarely best to do the anxious one first.

Is at-home grooming safe for a senior dog?

It is usually better than a salon for one. An arthritic dog cannot stand for ninety minutes, so the session is broken into stages with breaks and non-slip matting underfoot. Overgrown nails matter far more than a perfect coat at that age. Flag arthritis, heart or kidney trouble or any daily medication at booking.

How do I pay?

After the session, by cash or UPI. The price is the one quoted at booking — add-ons appear on the bill only if you agreed to them during the visit.
