Frequently asked questions

Is home grooming practical for busy working professionals?

Yes. Flexible time slots allow you to schedule grooming without disrupting work calls or office hours. The session runs independently while you continue your routine.

Do you offer de-shedding treatments for heavy-coated breeds?

Yes. Our de-shedding service removes loose undercoat effectively, especially useful for breeds prone to seasonal hair fall.

How do you handle grooming in high-rise societies?

We coordinate arrival timing in advance, carry portable equipment, and maintain strict cleanliness to ensure smooth sessions in apartment complexes.

Can puppies be groomed safely at home?

Yes. Early grooming in a familiar environment helps puppies adapt better and reduces fear associated with grooming tools.

What if my pet is afraid of dryers?

We use controlled airflow and gradual exposure techniques to keep the process comfortable.

Are monthly grooming plans available?

Yes, structured maintenance plans are available for consistent coat care throughout the year.

I work in Hinjewadi and only get back late. What is your last slot?

We run to 9 PM, and the last practical start for a full session is around 7 PM so the coat is properly dry before the groomer leaves. Wakad is one of our strongest areas for evening bookings precisely because of the Hinjewadi commute — those slots fill first, so book a few days ahead rather than the same evening.

Our society requires pets to be registered and asks for the service provider details. What do you give them?

The groomer carries identification and we can share the groomer name and contact ahead of the visit so your society office can log it before arrival. Several large Wakad townships now do this as standard. Tell us at booking if your gate needs the details in advance and we will send them rather than have the groomer held at the entrance.

The roads here flood badly in monsoon. Do sessions get cancelled?

Rarely cancelled, sometimes shifted. If the Wakad–Kalewadi stretch or the Dange Chowk approach is genuinely waterlogged we will call and move the slot rather than have a groomer arrive drenched with wet equipment. What we will not do is arrive late without telling you and then rush the session.

We have an imported breed that was groomed differently abroad. Can you match that?

Send a photograph of the previous clip when you book — it communicates far better than a breed-standard name does. Wakad has a lot of dogs that came with families relocating, and coats like Bichons, Cockers and Standard Poodles carry very different house styles. If a style is not achievable on the current coat length we will say so before starting rather than halfway through.

Can I book only the deshedding treatment without a haircut?

Yes. For double-coated dogs in a Wakad high-rise, deshedding is usually the thing actually solving your problem — fur on furniture — and a haircut does nothing for it. A deshedding blow-out with the bath is the standard combination we would suggest, and it is the one service where the difference is visible on the floor by the end.
