Frequently asked questions

Do you service the gated societies in Wanowrie?

Yes. We service all gated and non-gated residential areas across Wanowrie.

How do you handle dogs whose owners already do regular home brushing?

Home brushing is excellent maintenance and we're glad when owners do it. The professional session adds what home brushing can't — deep cleansing, proper blow-dry, ear checks, nail care, and coat conditioning. They work in complement.

Can I specify a particular grooming brief or trim style?

Yes. Detailed briefs are very welcome. Send a reference image and describe your preferences when booking. We discuss it before starting to ensure alignment.

What if my dog's coat has developed mats despite regular brushing?

Mats happen — particularly in areas prone to friction. We always attempt safe detangling before any clipping. We discuss with you before removing anything and explain what we find.

Is there a service difference between your first and subsequent sessions?

The first session is sometimes slightly longer as we assess the coat thoroughly and let the dog settle. By the second and third sessions, the routine is established and the session tends to run more smoothly.

Do you groom cats in Wanowrie?

Yes. Cat grooming is available in Wanowrie including for long-haired and Persian breeds.

Do groomers need clearance for Salunke Vihar and the cantonment-side addresses?

Only your gate's usual process for a service visitor. The groomer carries photo identification and we can send their name and number ahead so entry is logged before the slot. The cantonment-adjacent addresses in Wanowrie are stricter about pre-notification than the civilian societies, so arranging it beforehand is worth the two minutes.

Do you cover the NIBM Road and Undri side of Wanowrie?

Yes, along with Fatima Nagar, Salunke Vihar and the Hadapsar border stretch. Several societies here share names across phases, so mention your phase or tower at booking and send a pin — it is the usual reason a groomer arrives at the right complex and the wrong gate.

How do you adjust a session for a senior dog?

Shorter standing time, more breaks, non-slip matting under the paws throughout, and no positioning that forces a stiff joint. Water stays comfortably warm rather than hot, and we stop early if the dog tires rather than finishing the checklist. Tell us about arthritis, heart conditions or recent medication at booking so the groomer arrives planning for it.

What happens if the power goes out during the session?

Only the dryer needs electricity, so a cut pauses things rather than ending them. If your building has an inverter, tell the groomer which sockets are backed up. We will not leave a dog part-dried and go — if power is genuinely out we towel-dry down thoroughly and wait for it to return.

Is it harder to get a slot around Diwali and long weekends?

Considerably. Everyone wants the dog looking presentable on the same two or three days, and those slots go about a week ahead across Wanowrie and NIBM. If your dog is on a regular cycle, the simplest fix is booking the next session before the groomer leaves rather than competing for a festival slot later.
