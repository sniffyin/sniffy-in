Frequently asked questions

Do you provide premium grooming experiences at home?

Yes. Our grooming sessions are structured, detailed, and focused on coat health, hygiene, and overall wellness rather than quick cosmetic results.

Is your service suitable for show-quality or designer breeds?

Yes. Breed-specific trimming, coat shaping, and precision grooming are available for long-haired and designer breeds.

Do you offer conditioning treatments?

Yes. Hydration masks and coat conditioning options are available to improve shine and reduce dryness.

How do you maintain hygiene during grooming?

All tools are sanitized, fresh towels are used, and the area is cleaned after completion.

Can I set up a recurring grooming plan?

Yes. Structured maintenance plans ensure consistent coat care and hygiene.

Is the service discreet and quiet?

Yes. Our equipment is designed to operate smoothly without creating unnecessary disturbance.

Do you cover Undri, Mohammed Wadi and the townships off NIBM Road?

Yes — the NIBM Road spine, Undri, Mohammed Wadi and the large gated townships on either side. Several of these have multiple phases with the same name, so mention your phase or tower at booking. It is the single most common reason a groomer arrives at the right society and the wrong gate.

Our township requires a gate pass for service visits. How do we arrange that?

Tell us at booking and we will send you the groomer name and phone number ahead of the slot so you can raise the pass in advance. Most NIBM townships accept a resident-approved entry on the app or intercom. Without it the groomer waits at the gate, which eats into your slot rather than ours.

How do you care for an imported or long-coated breed that mats easily?

Line brushing rather than surface brushing, section by section, down to the skin — a surface comb glides over a coat that is already knitting underneath and tells you nothing. For coats like Bichons, Cockers and Shih Tzus we work the friction points first: behind the ears, the armpits, under the collar and around a harness line. Those four places account for most of the matting we find.

I need my dog prepared ahead of a show or a photo shoot. How should I time it?

Book the session three to five days before, not the day before. A freshly clipped coat sits slightly stiff and a bath strips some of the natural oil that gives a coat its lie and shine — both settle within a few days. Tell us it is for a show at booking so the groomer works to the coat length the ring needs rather than a practical house trim.

I travel often. Can my house staff or the security desk handle the session?

Yes, provided you have told us in advance who will be present and they can reach you by phone. Someone who knows the dog needs to be in the house. What we ask is that any decision that changes the dog — a clip-down for matting, skipping a step because the dog is distressed — is confirmed with you directly, not with a staff member on your behalf.
