Frequently asked questions

Do you service cantonment residential areas in Khadki?

Yes. We service all residential areas in Khadki including cantonment quarters and civilian neighbourhoods.

Can you handle working or high-drive dog breeds?

Yes. Calm, confident handling is the key to grooming working breeds successfully. We have experience with Labradors, German Shepherds, Dobermanns, and similar breeds.

What do you bring to the session?

All tools, shampoos, conditioners, dryers, towels, and sanitization supplies. You need to provide water access and a working space.

How do you handle very clean, well-maintained dogs who need light grooming?

We calibrate the session to the actual condition of the coat. A well-maintained dog may need less time on deshedding and more focus on nail care and ear cleaning. The session adapts to what's needed.

Is early morning booking available?

Yes. We offer morning slots from around 8am. Confirm the preferred time window when booking.

Do you groom cats in Khadki?

Yes. Cat grooming is available including for long-haired domestic breeds and Persians.

We live in military quarters. Do groomers need any special clearance to enter?

Nothing beyond what your gate normally requires for a service visitor. The groomer carries photo identification and we can send you their name and number in advance so you can log the entry before the slot. Cantonment gates are stricter than civilian societies about pre-notification, so it is worth doing rather than sorting it out at the barrier.

Should a German Shepherd be clipped down for the summer here?

No. A GSD's double coat insulates against heat as much as cold, and clipping it removes that protection and often grows back patchy and permanently coarser. The right answer is a thorough deshedding blow-out that strips the loose undercoat while leaving the guard hairs intact. It is the single most common request we talk owners out of.

Do you cover Dapodi, Sangvi and the Pimple Gurav side?

Yes, along with the Khadki cantonment area and the Aundh border. It is a compact, well-connected belt, so we can usually hold tighter arrival windows here than in the more spread-out outer areas.

Our dog is kennelled outdoors during the day. Does that change the session?

It changes the inspection more than the grooming. A dog with all-day outdoor exposure gets a fuller tick and skin check before anything else starts, and through monsoon we would suggest tightening the interval and keeping paw hair shorter. Outdoor-kept dogs also tend to have harder, more weathered pads, which is worth attention every visit.

Can we get a female groomer?

Say so at booking and we will schedule accordingly. It is a normal request and we would rather you asked than sat uncomfortable through a session in your own home. The one honest caveat is availability — it may mean a different day or a slightly wider slot window rather than the exact time you wanted.
