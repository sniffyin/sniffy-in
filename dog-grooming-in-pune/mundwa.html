Frequently asked questions

Is Mundwa fully covered by your service?

Yes. We cover all residential areas in Mundwa and surrounding localities including the newer apartment developments near Kalyani Nagar.

What is the smallest space you can set up in?

A bathroom with enough room for the groomer and the dog to manoeuvre comfortably works well. We've never had a space too small that we couldn't adapt to.

Can grooming help with tick prevention?

We check for ticks in every session and can use a mild anti-parasitic shampoo where needed. For tick-prone areas, regular grooming is one of the best early detection tools.

Is cat grooming available in Mundhwa too?

Yes. We groom cats as well — particularly longer-haired breeds that need regular coat maintenance and nail care.

What if my dog has never been groomed professionally before?

First sessions are often the most interesting. We take extra time to introduce tools gradually and build the dog's comfort. The home environment helps enormously here.

Are nail clipping and ear cleaning included in the base package?

Yes. Both are included as standard in our base grooming package. They're not extras you need to pay more for.

Does being close to the river change anything about grooming here?

Mainly the drying. The riverside stretch through Mundhwa holds humidity longer than areas further inland, and a coat that feels dry to the hand can still be damp at the skin. Our groomers dry down to the skin rather than to the touch here, and for thick-coated dogs that is the stage we spend the most time on.

Do you cover the Magarpatta and Keshav Nagar side as well as Mundhwa proper?

Yes, along with the Kharadi border stretch and the older Mundhwa gaon area. It is a compact belt to work in, which usually means we can offer tighter slot windows here than in the more spread-out parts of the city.

There is heavy construction around us. How often should we be grooming?

Closer to six weeks than eight for most coats. Construction dust is fine enough to settle into the undercoat rather than rest on top of it, and it leaves the coat dull and slightly abrasive to the touch. It is not a health emergency, but it is a real reason to shorten the gap while the building work continues.

We have a large dog in a small flat. Is there enough room to groom properly?

Almost always. The requirement is floor space for the dog to stand and turn, not a room to itself — a bathroom for the wash and any clear patch for the dry and trim is enough. For a big double-coated dog the deshedding stage is worth doing wherever it is easiest to sweep afterwards, which in a small flat is usually the balcony.

What is your latest evening slot in Mundhwa?

The last workable start is around 7 PM so the coat is fully dry before the groomer leaves. We run until 9 PM, but a session that begins at 8 either finishes rushed or finishes with a damp dog, and neither is something we will do. Evening slots here fill early in the week, so book a few days ahead.
