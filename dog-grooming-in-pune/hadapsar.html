Frequently asked questions

Do you cover all areas of Hadapsar including the older residential zones?

Yes. We service all of Hadapsar — from the tech corridor societies near Magarpatta to the older established residential areas.

Can you accommodate WFH-friendly slots around my meeting schedule?

Yes. We can work around your schedule. Once we're set up, the session runs independently and you don't need to supervise the whole time.

Is cat grooming available in Hadapsar?

Yes. We groom cats including Persians and domestic long-haired breeds across all of Hadapsar.

How often should a Doodle be groomed in Pune's climate?

Every four to five weeks. Pune's humidity accelerates matting in curly coats. Regular professional sessions plus home brushing between visits is the ideal combination.

What do you do if you find a skin issue or tick during grooming?

We alert you immediately, describe what we found, and recommend whether you should see a vet or monitor at home. We never remove embedded ticks ourselves — that requires a vet.

Are your products safe for Indie dogs with sensitive skin?

Yes. We carry gentle, fragrance-free options suited to sensitive skin types. Many of our rescued Indie clients use the hypoallergenic shampoo option.

Magarpatta and Amanora need a gate pass for service visitors. How do we arrange it?

Tell us at booking and we will send you the groomer's name and number ahead of the slot so you can raise the entry on the township app or with the gate. Both townships are strict about pre-approval, and without it the groomer waits at the barrier — which eats into your session rather than ours.

Which lift do groomers use in the township towers?

Whichever your building requires, including a service lift — everything fits in one shoulder bag so it makes no practical difference. The only thing worth flagging is if the service entrance is on a different side of the complex from the main gate, which is the usual reason a groomer loses ten minutes inside a large Hadapsar township.

What is your latest evening slot? I only get back after work.

The last workable start is around 7 PM, so the coat is fully dry before the groomer leaves. We run to 9 PM but a session starting at 8 either finishes rushed or finishes with a damp dog, and we will not do either. Evening slots in Hadapsar and Magarpatta go early in the week — book a few days ahead.

When can my puppy have its first session?

After the core vaccination course, usually around twelve to sixteen weeks — your vet can confirm where your puppy is in the schedule. Keep the first visit short and deliberately easy: paws and ears handled, a comb through, the dryer heard from across the room. The aim is a puppy that accepts handling, not a finished haircut.

Someone at home is allergic to dog hair. Is deshedding worth booking?

Yes, more than most other services. The trigger for most people is dander riding on shed undercoat rather than the hair itself, so removing that undercoat on a schedule instead of letting it drop around the flat makes a genuine difference. It manages the problem rather than eliminating it, but for a shedding breed in an apartment it is the most effective single thing available.
