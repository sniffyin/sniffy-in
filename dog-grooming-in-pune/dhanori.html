Frequently asked questions

Is at-home grooming better than taking my pet to a salon?

For many dogs, yes. Grooming at home removes the stress of car rides, waiting areas, and unfamiliar animals. In growing residential areas with busy roads, avoiding travel can significantly reduce anxiety and make the session smoother and safer for your dog.

What does a complete grooming session include?

A standard session includes bathing with breed-appropriate shampoo, blow drying, brushing, nail trimming, ear cleaning, sanitary trimming, and optional haircut styling. Before starting, the groomer evaluates coat condition and discusses the most suitable approach with you.

Can you manage heavy shedding and dusty coat buildup?

Yes. Environmental dust and seasonal coat changes can cause excessive shedding. Our de-shedding process removes loose undercoat safely while improving skin health and shine, helping keep your home cleaner for longer.

Is your equipment safe to use inside apartments?

Absolutely. Our grooming setup is compact, controlled, and designed for indoor use. We manage water usage carefully and clean the working area after the session to maintain hygiene.

How frequently should I schedule grooming?

Most dogs benefit from grooming every 4–6 weeks. Regular sessions prevent matting, reduce odor, and help detect early skin or ear issues before they become serious.

Do you handle senior or anxious dogs gently?

Yes. We follow a low-stress approach that includes calm handling, breaks when needed, and minimal restraint. Senior dogs especially benefit from familiar home environments during grooming.

Do you cover Porwal Road, Kalas and Tingre Nagar as well as Dhanori proper?

Yes, all of them, including the newer society clusters along Porwal Road and the Kalas–Vishrantwadi stretch. Dhanori has expanded faster than its road names have settled, so if your society is known locally by the builder name rather than the area, just send us a pin and we will confirm the slot.

My dog reacts badly to aircraft noise from the airport approach. Does that affect the session?

It can, and it is worth telling us at booking. Dogs in Dhanori and Kalas live under the Lohegaon approach path, and a low overflight mid-blow-dry startles a dog that was settling. Our groomers pause rather than push through, and where a dog is very sound-reactive we schedule the dryer stage for a quieter window and use a lower fan speed for longer.

Our society water supply is on a fixed timing. Does the groomer need water the whole session?

No — the actual water use is short. The groomer needs a running tap for the bath and rinse stages, roughly fifteen to twenty minutes of the visit, not the full ninety. If your supply is timed, book a slot inside that window or keep a couple of filled buckets ready and the session runs exactly the same.

I am flying my dog out and need him groomed before travel. How far ahead should that be?

Two to three days before the flight, not the morning of it. A dog needs to be fully dry to the skin and settled before a crate journey, and a bath on travel day leaves a damp undercoat in an air-conditioned hold. Nails short and paw hair trimmed matter more for crate comfort than a fresh haircut does.

Can the session happen in the society clubhouse or pet area instead of my flat?

Yes, if your society permits it and there is a tap and a plug point. Some Dhanori societies prefer it and it suits large or heavy-shedding dogs. The one thing that changes is distraction — an open common area with other residents and dogs passing is harder for a nervous dog than a closed bathroom is.
