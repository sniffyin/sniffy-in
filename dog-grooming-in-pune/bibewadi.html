Frequently asked questions

Do you work with dogs that have been in the family for many years?

Yes. Long-term family dogs are among our most appreciated clients. We treat every senior dog with the patience and respect their age and relationship with your family warrants.

Is home grooming suitable for older dogs who don't travel well?

It's ideal. Senior dogs with arthritis, mobility issues, or travel anxiety benefit enormously from staying in their home environment for grooming.

What shampoo do you use for dogs with sensitive or ageing skin?

We carry mild, fragrance-free options for sensitive skin. Ageing dogs often have drier, more delicate skin and we select products accordingly.

How do you handle grooming during monsoon?

We continue operating during monsoon. For dogs exposed to damp conditions, we use an anti-fungal rinse as standard precaution.

Is regular grooming important even for short-haired dogs?

Yes. Short-haired dogs still shed, develop ear issues, and grow nails. Regular grooming provides health monitoring that is valuable for any coat type.

Can I observe the full session?

Yes. You are welcome to watch throughout. Some dogs actually do better with the owner present; others do slightly better with a little space. We let the dog tell us which they prefer.

Do you cover Sahakarnagar, Parvati and the Sinhagad Road side?

Yes, along with the Katraj and Kondhwa ends of Bibewadi. It is a dense, central-south belt and easy for us to reach, so arrival windows here are usually tighter and more reliable than in the outer townships.

We walk our dog on Parvati Hill daily. Is there anything specific to check?

Grass seeds and burrs alongside the usual tick check. The hill paths shed seed heads that lodge between the toes and inside the ear canal, and left alone they can work under the skin. Our groomers check the webbing between every toe and both ears as standard — for a dog walking Parvati every morning that is one of the more useful things a session does.

Our building is old with narrow stairs and no lift. Does that work for an older dog?

It works, but we plan around the dog rather than the building. If your senior dog does not do stairs comfortably, we set up on the ground floor and work from filled buckets rather than bringing them up and down for a bath. Old flooring is also genuinely slippery for an arthritic dog, so the groomer lays non-slip matting throughout.

Our dog has a heart condition. Is a full session safe?

Often yes, with a shorter session and more breaks — but tell us at booking and tell us what your vet has said. We shorten the standing time, avoid positions that stress the chest, keep the water comfortably warm rather than hot, and stop early if the dog tires. If we think the session is not safe for your dog on the day, we will say so and reschedule rather than push through.

Can I get a nail trim on its own for our senior dog?

Yes, and for an older dog it is often the sensible choice. A short maintenance visit covering nails, ears and paw hair is available below package price. Overgrown nails change how an arthritic dog loads its joints, so keeping them short matters considerably more than bathing an elderly dog on a fixed schedule does.
