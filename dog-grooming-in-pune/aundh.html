Frequently asked questions

Can I set up a fixed monthly recurring grooming slot in Aundh?

Yes. We can set up a recurring monthly booking so your slot is reserved and you don't need to rebook each time. Very popular with Aundh clients.

Do you follow a specific brief for haircut styles?

Yes. Send a reference image or describe the style you want when booking. We discuss it before starting to make sure we're aligned.

How does home grooming compare to salon grooming for quality?

Home grooming offers more concentrated attention — one groomer, one dog, full focus. There's no queue, no cage time, and no disruption from other animals. For most dogs this produces a calmer session and better results.

What happens if my dog needs a long session due to heavy coat condition?

We will communicate this proactively. If the coat requires significantly more time than standard, we discuss options with you rather than rushing to fit a time window.

Is early morning booking available in Aundh?

Yes. Slots from 8am are available. Confirm the preferred window when booking.

Are all grooming products pet-safe?

Yes. We use veterinarian-approved, pet-specific products. Hypoallergenic options are available for dogs with documented sensitivities.

Parking is tight in the older lanes here. Does the groomer need a spot?

No. Groomers travel by two-wheeler with the whole kit in a shoulder bag, which is why this works in the older parts of Aundh at all. Nothing is unloaded at your gate and no four-wheeler needs a bay — the same reason we can work the narrow lanes off Bremen Chowk without difficulty.

We are boarding our dog next week. Should we groom before or after?

Before, by a few days. A dog going into boarding with a clean, mat-free coat and short nails is easier for the kennel to manage and far less likely to come back with a coat problem. Grooming immediately after boarding is also reasonable if the stay was long, but doing it beforehand is what actually prevents trouble.

Can I book only a nail trim and ear clean?

Yes — a short maintenance visit covering nails, ears and a paw-hair trim is available below package price. Ask for it by name at booking. For a short-coated dog it is frequently all that is genuinely needed between full sessions, and it avoids bathing a dog more often than its coat requires.

Can we request a female groomer?

Yes, just say so when you book. It is a routine request and we would much rather accommodate it than have you uncomfortable during a home visit. The only caveat is scheduling — it may mean a different day or a wider arrival window rather than your first-choice slot.

We think there are fleas in the house, not just on the dog. Does grooming fix that?

Grooming clears the dog, not the house, and it is important to be honest about that. A tick and flea treatment during the session removes what is on the animal, but eggs in bedding, sofas and floor cracks will re-infest within days. Treat the dog and the environment in the same week — wash all bedding hot and vacuum thoroughly — or you will be doing this repeatedly.
