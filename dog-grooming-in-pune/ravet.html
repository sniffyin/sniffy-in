Breeds We Groom in Ravet — And What First-Time Owners Need to Know

A growing community, a growing dog roster.

We see a lot of Labradors (always), a solid contingent of Huskies from the newer apartment blocks, Pomeranians, Indies, and increasingly, Doodles. First-time owners are often surprised by how breed-specific grooming actually needs to be.

Here's our approach to the breeds we groom most in Ravet:

  • Labrador grooming in Ravet — Short coats that hide how much undercoat Labs actually have until you start the deshedding process. We always explain this to first-time Lab owners — the amount of loose coat that comes out in a proper deshedding session is significant and genuinely improves the dog's comfort.
  • Husky grooming in Ravet — Pune's climate is genuinely hard on Huskies and many first-time Husky owners in Ravet are surprised by the shedding volume. We do a proper blow-out deshedding session — never clipping or shaving, which would permanently damage the double coat.
  • Pomeranian grooming in Ravet — Small, opinionated, and prone to matting in Pune's humidity. We take time at the start to let the dog settle, then work through the double coat carefully and deliver a clean rounded finish.
  • Doodle grooming in Ravet — First-time Doodle owners often don't realise how quickly the curly non-shedding coat mats. We explain this at the first session and set up a grooming frequency plan that prevents the coat from becoming unmanageable.
  • Indie dog grooming in Ravet — Many Ravet clients have adopted Indies from local rescue organisations. These dogs are often confident and social, which makes grooming straightforward — we still do a thorough tick and skin check every time.
  • German Shepherd grooming in Ravet — Double coats, high-shedding, and sometimes wary of strangers. We give GSDs time to settle into our presence before beginning and handle the session with quiet confidence throughout.

Frequently asked questions

Is this service suitable for first-time dog owners?

Very much so. We walk you through the session and explain what we observe about your dog's coat and health. First sessions are often the most educational for new dog parents.

How often should I book grooming for a Labrador or Husky?

Monthly is ideal for both breeds, especially in Pune's climate. Labs shed year-round and Huskies blow their coat seasonally — regular professional sessions manage both effectively.

Does the session cover all of Ravet including the Tathawade border areas?

Yes. We cover all of Ravet and the surrounding localities including Tathawade and Pimple Saudagar borders.

Can grooming help reduce the fur around my apartment?

Significantly. A proper deshedding session removes far more loose coat than home brushing and reduces daily shedding noticeably for several weeks afterward.

What if my dog has never been groomed professionally?

We take a patient, gradual approach — introducing tools one at a time and allowing breaks. Home environments make first grooming experiences much better than salon ones.

Is weekend booking available?

Yes. Weekend slots are available but fill faster than weekdays. Book a few days in advance for the best timing options.

Do you cover Punawale, Tathawade and the Kiwale side as well as Ravet?

Yes, the whole newer township belt through Ravet, Punawale, Kiwale and across to Tathawade. Many of these societies are known by builder name rather than street, and several share names across phases, so mention your phase or tower when booking.

New townships here often have irregular water supply. How do we plan around it?

Keep three or four buckets filled before the slot if your supply is timed. The wash and rinse stages together are only about twenty minutes of a ninety-minute visit, so stored water covers a session comfortably. Just tell the groomer on arrival so they plan the rinse rather than discover the tap is dry mid-lather.

Someone in the family is allergic to dog hair. Does grooming actually help?

A proper deshedding session helps more than most people expect, because what triggers most reactions is dander carried on shed undercoat rather than the hair itself. Pulling that loose undercoat out on a schedule, rather than letting it drop around the flat, makes a real difference. It manages the problem rather than solving it — but for a double-coated dog in an apartment it is the single most effective thing available.

Our tower has a service lift and a separate entrance. Is that a problem?

No — the groomer follows whatever your building requires and carries everything in one bag. The only thing worth flagging at booking is if the service entrance is on a different side of the complex from the main gate, which is where a groomer typically loses ten minutes in a large Ravet township.

Power cuts happen here. What if the electricity goes during the session?

The dryer is the only stage that needs power, so a cut pauses the session rather than ending it. Tell the groomer which sockets are on the inverter if you have one. We will not leave a dog part-dried — if power is genuinely out, we towel-dry thoroughly and wait rather than call it done.
