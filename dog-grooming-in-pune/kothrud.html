Frequently asked questions

Is home grooming beneficial for senior dogs?

Yes. Older dogs are more comfortable when groomed in familiar surroundings, avoiding stressful travel and loud salon environments.

Can regular grooming prevent health issues?

Routine sessions help detect early signs of ear infections, skin irritation, and nail overgrowth before they worsen.

Do you groom both cats and dogs?

Yes. We provide complete grooming solutions for both species with techniques adapted to their temperament.

What if my pet resists grooming?

We follow a calm, patient approach and allow breaks to build trust gradually.

How much space is required for setup?

A small balcony or bathroom area with water access is usually sufficient.

Do you provide haircut styling services?

Yes. Customized haircut styles are available based on breed standards and owner preferences.

Many buildings here are older walk-ups. Does a fourth-floor flat with no lift work?

For most dogs, yes — the groomer carries a shoulder kit, not equipment that needs a lift. The question is your dog, not the stairs for us. A heavy or arthritic dog that struggles with four flights should not be brought down and up for a bath, so if that is the situation we would rather work in a ground-floor space or a neighbour's courtyard.

How does booking work during Ganpati and the festival weeks?

Book at least a week ahead. Kothrud fills early and the roads around Karve Road and Paud Road become genuinely unpredictable during processions, which affects our ability to hold slot timings. If your dog is noise-sensitive, a session in the quiet week before the festival is far kinder than one during it.

What is the best slot to avoid Paud Road and Karve Road traffic?

Late morning, roughly 11 AM to 1 PM, is the most reliable window for a groomer to reach Kothrud on time. Peak morning and the 6 to 8 PM stretch are where slot timings slip most, and we would rather book you into a window we can actually hold than promise a 7 PM arrival and call you at 7:20.

We adopted an indie who is nervous around new people. How do you start with a dog like that?

Slowly, and often without touching the dog at all for the first several minutes. The groomer sits, lets the dog approach and inspect the kit, and starts with the least invasive handling — usually a brush along the back rather than paws or ears. A first session with a rescue may not complete everything, and we would rather do eighty percent calmly than all of it by force.

Our water comes at fixed hours. Should we book around that?

It helps, but it is not essential. The wash and rinse stages together are around twenty minutes of the visit, so four filled buckets cover a medium dog comfortably. Just tell the groomer on arrival so they are rinsing from stored water deliberately rather than discovering the tap is dry mid-lather.
