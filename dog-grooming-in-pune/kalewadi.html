Frequently asked questions

Do you cover all societies in Kalewadi?

Yes. We service all residential societies and independent homes across Kalewadi.

What should I expect during the first session?

We start with a brief coat assessment and a few minutes to let your dog get comfortable with the groomer's presence. Then we proceed through the session step by step and brief you fully at the end.

Can grooming help with seasonal shedding?

Yes significantly. Professional deshedding sessions remove far more loose coat than home brushing. Most owners report a meaningful reduction in daily fur accumulation for two to three weeks after a proper session.

Is cat grooming available in Kalewadi?

Yes. We groom cats in Kalewadi including domestic short-haired, long-haired, and Persian breeds.

How do you handle a dog that pulls or fidgets during grooming?

We use positioning and patient handling rather than restraint wherever possible. Most fidgeting reduces once the dog understands the grooming routine — particularly after the second or third session.

Are evening slots available?

Yes. We offer evening slots until around 7pm. Confirm the preferred window when booking.

Our building is a walk-up with no lift and we have a large dog. Is that workable?

For us, yes — everything the groomer needs fits in one shoulder bag. The question is whether your dog manages the stairs comfortably day to day. If it does, nothing changes. If it does not, we would rather work on the ground floor or in the compound than have a heavy dog climb three floors before standing still for an hour.

How bad is the Kalewadi Phata traffic for slot timings?

Bad enough that we plan around it. Peak morning and the 6 to 8 PM stretch through the Phata are where arrival windows slip most, so we would rather book you late morning or early afternoon and actually arrive on time. If evening suits you better, expect a wider window rather than a precise minute.

We have a small flat with one shared bathroom. Is there enough space?

Yes. The bathroom is needed for perhaps twenty minutes of the visit for the wash and rinse; the rest happens on any clear patch of floor with the groomer's own mats laid down. Small flats across Kalewadi are a large share of what we do — the constraint is almost never space, it is whether the dog can stand and turn.

The lanes here flood in heavy monsoon. Do you still come?

Usually yes, occasionally rescheduled. If the approach to your building is genuinely waterlogged we will call and move the slot rather than send a groomer wading through it with electrical equipment. Rescheduling costs you nothing — a groomer arriving soaked with a wet dryer bag helps neither of us.

My parents will be home, not me. Do your groomers speak Marathi?

Yes — our groomers work in Marathi and Hindi as well as English, and in Kalewadi that comes up constantly. Tell us at booking if an elderly family member will be handling the visit, and we will make sure the groomer and the household share a language for the instructions that actually matter.
