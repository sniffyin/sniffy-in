Frequently asked questions

Does your service cover the newer plotted developments in Dhayari?

Yes. We cover all residential areas in Dhayari including newer societies and plotted developments.

Can outdoor dogs or dogs with access to gardens be groomed effectively at home?

Yes. We do a deeper cleanse for dogs with outdoor access and include paw pad checks for any cuts or debris.

What if my dog sheds heavily — can one session actually make a difference?

Yes. A professional deshedding session removes significantly more loose undercoat than home brushing can. Most owners notice a marked reduction in daily fur accumulation for two to three weeks after a session.

Do you require anything from me before the groomer arrives?

Just a water-accessible space and a quick bathroom break for your dog. Bring any existing grooming products if you have preferences — otherwise we bring everything.

Is at-home grooming available on weekends in Dhayari?

Yes. Weekend slots are available though they fill faster. We recommend booking a few days in advance for preferred timings.

How long between sessions is appropriate?

Every 4–6 weeks for most breeds. High-shedding breeds in summer may benefit from monthly sessions.

We are on borewell water and it runs hard. Does that affect the bath?

It does, and it is a real issue across the Dhayari and Narhe side. Hard water leaves mineral residue that dulls a coat and can leave the skin itchy even after a proper rinse. Our groomers rinse longer where the water is hard and use a conditioning rinse to counter it. If your coat has looked dull after home baths, this is usually why.

Do you cover Kirkatwadi, Nanded City and the plots toward Sinhagad Road?

Yes, along with Narhe and the newer plotted developments through Dhayari. It is a spread-out area with a lot of similarly named lanes, so a location pin with the booking is worth thirty seconds of your time and saves the groomer a call from somewhere near your gate.

Our dog sleeps outside in the compound at night. Does that change the grooming plan?

It changes what we look for more than what we do. A dog with unrestricted night access to a compound gets a much more thorough tick inspection — between the toes, inside the ears, along the jawline and under the tail — before anything else starts. Through monsoon we would also suggest shorter paw hair so mud carries less into the house.

It gets very hot on the terrace in summer. Can the session still be done outdoors?

Early morning or evening, yes. Midday on an exposed Dhayari terrace in April is genuinely too hot for a dog standing still for an hour, and heat plus a dryer is a bad combination. If outdoors is your preference for a heavy shedder, book the first or last slot of the day and we will work in shade.

We adopted an indie from the neighbourhood. Is grooming necessary for a street-origin dog?

Less bathing than a pedigree coat needs, but the same tick and skin checks — arguably more of them. Indies generally have practical, self-maintaining coats and over-bathing does them no favours. What earns its keep is a real inspection every session, nail trimming, and ear checks, since these are the dogs most likely to have picked something up outdoors.
