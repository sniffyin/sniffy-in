Why it matters

Grooming in Pune is a bit different

Pune seasons

Pune's climate is harder on coats than most people realise

Dust for nine months, then three of real humidity.

The city runs warm and dusty for most of the year, and then spends three months being genuinely humid during monsoon. That combination — dust buildup in summer, damp fur in monsoon — makes skin and coat problems more likely here than in cities with more moderate weather. Dogs pick up road dust every walk, oils accumulate faster in the heat, and wet fur that doesn't dry properly in closed apartments leads to fungal issues we see regularly.

At your doorstep

Home grooming isn't a luxury here — it makes practical sense

A salon trip across Pune is half a day gone.

Pune traffic is real. Driving a dog to a salon in Kothrud when you live in Wagholi, waiting an hour or two, driving back — that's easily a half day gone. And the dog has spent those hours in a cage surrounded by strangers and other animals. At-home grooming removes all of that. The groomer comes to you, works in your space, your dog stays calm, and you're not stuck driving anywhere.

Same shampoos, same tools, same approach. We don't have franchise inconsistency because we're not a franchise — it's one team with one standard across Pune.

Indie & rescue dogs

Indie and rescue dog grooming is a bigger part of this city than most

Pune has one of India's largest adopted-Indie populations, and grooming them isn't a smaller version of a purebred groom.

A large share of the dogs we groom across Pune are Indies — adopted off the street, out of a shelter, or taken in as a resident dog by a housing society. Indie dog grooming and rescue dog grooming aren't a discount version of purebred grooming: coats vary widely from dog to dog, many have spent real time outdoors and carry heavier tick loads than an apartment-raised Labrador, and plenty of adopted dogs are still building trust with being handled by a stranger at all. We treat that as its own category — a longer first visit built around letting the dog settle before any tool comes out, a proper tick and skin check given the time many have spent outside, and no assumption that a dog with an unknown history will sit still the way a puppy raised indoors from eight weeks does.

Breed-wise care

Breed-specific dog grooming: a Shih Tzu, a Lab and a Pug need three different sessions

Coat type decides the tools, the timing and how often you should book.

The dogs we groom across Pune are mostly Shih Tzus, Labradors, Golden Retrievers, Pomeranians, Pugs, German Shepherds, Poodles and Indies, and a single 'dog grooming' routine doesn't suit them all. A Shih Tzu or Poodle has a coat that keeps growing and mats at the skin if it isn't brushed, so it needs a haircut every few weeks. A Labrador or Golden Retriever sheds a thick undercoat that a bath and a proper blow-dry will loosen, and shaving it only damages the coat. A Pug or Beagle has a short coat that needs a bath, clean skin folds and ears rather than a haircut. Tell us the breed, size and coat when you book and the groomer arrives with the right brushes and clippers, and honest advice on how often to come back instead of a one-size-fits-all schedule.

Puppies & seniors

Puppy first grooms and senior dog care: the two ages that need a slower session

A nervous puppy and a stiff ten-year-old both do better at home than on a salon table.

Puppy grooming in Pune usually starts with a first bath and a lot of curiosity, and the first visit should be treated as an introduction rather than a full groom: the sound of the dryer, a comb through the coat, paws handled, a short bath, treats, and an early finish if the puppy has had enough. Puppies who meet a groomer calmly in their own living room tend to carry that confidence into every visit after, which is the whole point. Ask your vet about vaccination timing before booking, and tell us the puppy's age when you do.

At the other end, senior dog grooming is about joints and patience. An older Labrador or Spitz with arthritis shouldn't be made to stand for an hour on a slippery bathroom floor, so we keep sessions shorter, use warm rather than hot water, give breaks, and work around sore hips instead of through them. Both age groups pay the same ₹799 Spa Bath, ₹999 Hair Cut or ₹1299 Spa Bath + Hair Cut as any other dog; what changes is the pace, not the price. Pet parents in Kothrud, Aundh and Baner book these gentler first and later-life visits regularly.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover all areas of Pune?

We offer doorstep dog grooming across Pune, including Wakad, Wagholi, Kothrud, Shivajinagar, Kondhwa, NIBM, Dhanori and Pimpri-Chinchwad. We're adding more areas regularly — if you're not in our coverage zone yet, you can still fill the form and we'll let you know when we reach you.

What does a standard session include?

Bath with shampoo and conditioner, blow dry, brushing, nail clipping, eye and ear cleaning, paw massage, mouth spray, and a light perfume spritz. For breeds with specific coat needs we adjust technique accordingly.

How long does a session take?

Between 1.5 and 3 hours depending on breed, coat type and temperament. Smaller short-haired dogs are quicker. Large breeds with thick coats take longer. Your groomer will give you a time estimate when they arrive.

What if my dog is anxious or has never been groomed before?

Tell us when you book. We'll send a groomer who's experienced with anxious dogs and allow extra time. First sessions are always slower — we let the dog settle, start with the least invasive things, and take breaks when needed. Most dogs are significantly more relaxed by their second or third session.

How is this different from a salon?

At a salon your dog is caged between steps, surrounded by other animals, and sharing one groomer's attention with multiple dogs. At home there's no travel stress, no cage, no other animals, and one groomer focused entirely on your dog for the whole session.

How often should I book?

Depends on the breed. Short-haired dogs: every 6–8 weeks. Medium coats like Goldens: every 4–6 weeks. Double-coated breeds like Huskies: every 3–4 weeks. Continuously growing coats like Poodles or Shih Tzus: every 3–4 weeks for a bath, haircut every 6–8 weeks. In monsoon, bump all of these slightly more frequent.

What do you need from my flat to run a session?

A tap — bathroom or balcony is fine — one working plug point for the dryer, and enough clear floor for the dog to stand and turn. That is genuinely it. The groomer arrives by two-wheeler with clippers, blades, dryer, shampoos, towels, mats and disposables in one kit. No van, no parking slot, no water connection of ours.

Does my housing society need to approve the groomer’s entry?

In practice, no. A groomer arriving on foot with a shoulder bag is treated like any delivery or home-service visit at the gate — we have worked in large gated townships across Wakad, Kharadi, Ravet and NIBM without a single society objection. Some gates ask the resident to confirm on the intercom, so keep your phone nearby around the slot time.

Is tick and flea treatment included, or is it extra?

A tick check is included in every session — the groomer inspects between the toes, inside the ears, along the jawline and under the tail before anything else starts. The treatment itself is an add-on at ₹149, and we only suggest it if we actually find something. Finding ticks and telling you costs nothing.

What happens if the groomer is running late?

You get a call, not silence. Pune traffic being what it is, a groomer stuck between Hinjewadi and Kothrud at 6 PM will let the coordinator know and we pass it on with a revised arrival window. If the delay pushes past what works for you, we reschedule to another slot at no charge.

What is included in a dog spa bath in Pune, and how is it different from a dog haircut?

A dog spa bath is our Spa Bath at ₹799 — premium shampoo, nourishing coat conditioner, deshedding and a full blow-dry to the skin, with nothing trimmed. A dog haircut is our Hair Cut at ₹999 — full body trimming or a full body haircut at a length you pick, finished with a loose-hair blow-out. Book both together as a dog full grooming session and it is ₹1299 rather than ₹1798.

What counts as dog basic grooming versus dog full grooming?

Basic grooming is the bath side of the job: wash, conditioner, deshed, dry, plus nails, ears and a paw check. That is our ₹799 Spa Bath. Full grooming adds the haircut, which is our ₹1299 Spa Bath + Hair Cut. The distinction people miss is drying — some operators in this city sell a 'basic' that skips the blow-dry, and in Pune humidity an under-dried coat is the single most common cause of the skin problems we get called about.

Do you do a dog dry bath or waterless grooming?

Not as a bookable package, and we would rather tell you why than take the money. A dry bath works foam or powder through the coat and brushes it out — it covers odour without removing what is causing it, and a coat that is never rinsed and dried properly in this climate is where fungal trouble begins. If your dog genuinely cannot be bathed right now — stitches, a frail senior, a skin condition your vet is treating — we will do a dry tidy-up inside a normal session rather than sell it as one.

How do I pick the best dog grooming service in Pune?

Judge it on four things rather than on the word 'best'. Is the whole price published up front? Is a Saint Bernard charged the same as a Pug? Do they tell you which groomer is coming before he arrives? And will they refuse work your dog does not need — talking you out of shaving a double coat in April, for instance? A dog grooming service in Pune that clears all four is a safe booking. Ours are ₹799/₹999/₹1299 published, no size surcharge, groomer named at booking, and we refuse the shave.

Do you do senior dog grooming, and is it priced differently?

Same three prices, a different session. Senior dog grooming means shorter standing time, breaks built in, non-slip matting underfoot, warm rather than hot water, and no position that forces a stiff hip or shoulder. Nails matter more than a tidy coat at this age, because an overgrown nail changes how an arthritic joint loads. Tell us about arthritis, heart or kidney conditions, or recent surgery at booking, and if a full groom looks like too much on the day we will do only what is needed and say so.

Do you groom independent houses and bungalows, or only apartments?

Both, and bungalows are often easier — an outdoor tap and open space suit a large double-coated dog better than a flat bathroom does. What does change is the tick check: dogs with garden access and long grass need a more thorough inspection, and in areas with heavy tree cover the tick season runs longer than it does in the concrete parts of the city.
