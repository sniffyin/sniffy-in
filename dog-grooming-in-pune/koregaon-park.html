Frequently asked questions

Can you groom in an outdoor garden setting?

Yes. We adapt to outdoor spaces including gardens and terraces. This is often ideal for larger breeds and works especially well in Koregaon Park bungalow properties.

Do you have experience with rare or uncommon breeds?

Yes. Our groomers have experience with a wide range of breeds including Weimaraners, Vizslas, Standard Poodles, Ridgebacks, and various other European breeds. Contact us in advance if you have a particularly unusual breed.

Can I specify a grooming style or clip for my Poodle?

Yes. We groom Poodles to the owner's specified style. Send a reference image when booking and we'll discuss before starting.

How do you handle large and powerful breeds?

With confidence and calm authority. Dogs read handler energy closely — a groomer who is calm and assured produces a calmer, more cooperative dog. We never use force; we use positioning and patient handling.

Is cat grooming available in Koregaon Park?

Yes. We groom cats including Persians and long-haired domestic cats. Cat sessions are quieter and require a specific patient approach we are trained for.

What is the frequency recommendation for a Standard Poodle?

Every four weeks for a Poodle in full coat. Their non-shedding coat grows continuously and mats quickly without regular maintenance.

The bungalow lanes here are narrow and parking is tight. Does that affect the visit?

No. Groomers arrive by two-wheeler with everything in a shoulder bag, which is the reason this service works on the lanes off North Main Road and the numbered lanes at all. No van, no bay, and nothing that needs to be unloaded at your gate.

Does the humidity near the river affect how a coat should be dried?

It affects how long it takes, not how it is done. This stretch holds moisture longer than most of the city, and a thick coat that feels dry on the surface can still be damp against the skin. We dry to the skin rather than to the touch, and for a Retriever or a Husky here that stage takes noticeably longer than it would in drier parts of Pune.

I travel a lot. Can my house staff manage the appointment?

Yes, provided someone the dog trusts is present and you are contactable. The limit is decisions: if the coat turns out to be matted enough that clipping down is the kinder option, that call comes to you by phone, not to whoever happens to be at home. We would rather hold the session for five minutes than change your dog on someone else's authority.

We are moving our dog abroad. What should be done beforehand?

Bath two to three days ahead so the coat is fully dry before the crate, nails taken short, and paw and sanitary hair trimmed — the practical things that make a long flight less uncomfortable. Leave the cosmetic trim alone if the coat is in good order. The tight scheduling should be around the vet health certificate, not around the grooming.

Our senior dog struggles with the stairs in an old bungalow. How do you work around that?

We come to the dog rather than moving the dog. If the bathroom is upstairs and your dog should not be doing stairs, we work on the ground floor from filled buckets and use non-slip matting throughout — old bungalow flooring is genuinely slippery for an arthritic dog. Tell us at booking so the groomer arrives planning for it.
