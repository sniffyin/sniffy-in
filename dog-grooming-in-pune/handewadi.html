Frequently asked questions

Does your service cover the newer societies in Handewadi?

Yes. We service all residential areas in Handewadi including newer society developments and older plotted properties.

Do you check for ticks in every session?

Yes. A full tick check covering the ears, neck, underbelly, tail base, and between the toes is part of every session. We flag anything we find immediately.

Is grooming suitable for dogs that have never been groomed before?

Absolutely. We take a gradual, patient approach for first-time grooms — introducing tools slowly and allowing breaks. Home environments make this much more successful than salon settings.

What is your anti-parasitic treatment option for tick-prone dogs?

We can use a mild anti-parasitic shampoo as part of the session for dogs with known tick exposure. Mention this when booking and we'll prepare accordingly.

Can I book for multiple dogs in one visit?

Yes. Multi-dog sessions are available. Mention all dogs when booking so we plan the session time correctly.

How does pricing work for different breed sizes?

Pricing varies by size and coat complexity. We give a transparent quote when you contact us with your dog's breed and size. No surprises.

Do you cover Undri, Mohammed Wadi and the societies toward Saswad Road?

Yes, along with the Handewadi Road belt and the Kondhwa side. This area has grown quickly and several societies share builder names across different phases, so mention your phase or tower at booking and send a pin — it saves the groomer a call from your gate.

The highway traffic throws up a lot of dust. Does that shorten the grooming cycle?

It does, by roughly a couple of weeks for most coats. Road dust off the Pune–Solapur stretch is fine enough to settle into the undercoat rather than sit on top of it, and it leaves the coat dull and gritty to the hand. Six weeks generally holds condition better here than the eight a coat type alone would suggest.

Our new society still has irregular water supply. Is that enough for a session?

Easily. The wash and rinse stages together are around twenty minutes of the visit — three or four filled buckets cover a medium dog comfortably. If your supply is timed, either book inside that window or keep buckets ready and tell the groomer on arrival so they work from stored water deliberately.

We keep a large guard breed. How do you approach a protective dog?

With an escorted introduction rather than a cold one. Tell us the breed and temperament at booking, and meet the groomer at the gate and bring them in yourself — a territorial dog reads an escorted stranger very differently from one who appears at the door. From there the groomer works slowly and lets the dog set the pace on the first visit.

Are weekend slots available here?

Yes, Saturday and Sunday at the same 9 AM to 9 PM hours. Weekends are the busiest window in this belt because most households here work a standard week, so book four or five days ahead rather than trying on the Friday and hoping.
