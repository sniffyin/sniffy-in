Frequently asked questions

Can I book for a weekday morning to fit around my work calls?

Yes. We offer morning, afternoon, and evening slots including weekdays. Just pick what works and confirm — we'll be there.

How do Doodle coats need to be maintained between sessions?

Daily brushing with a slicker brush and occasional comb-through prevents the curly coat from matting. We give specific guidance at the end of every Doodle session.

Does grooming help with dust accumulation in the coat?

Yes significantly. Deep-cleanse shampoos and proper blow-dry remove settled dust from the undercoat that a surface rinse would leave behind.

Is at-home grooming available for same-day bookings?

Subject to availability. We recommend booking 24–48 hours in advance for the best slot options, though we accommodate same-day where possible.

Do you handle dogs that have had bad salon experiences before?

Yes. Dogs with previous salon anxiety almost always respond better in a home environment. We give extra time at the start of the session for the dog to settle.

What happens if my dog has a mat that needs clipping?

We always discuss this with you before clipping. We attempt to detangle safely first and only recommend clipping if the mat is too tight to safely brush out.

The bypass and the EON stretch flood in heavy rain. Do sessions still happen?

Usually yes, occasionally shifted. If the Kharadi bypass or the approach to your society is genuinely waterlogged, we call and move the slot rather than send a groomer through it with electrical equipment. A rescheduled session costs you nothing; a groomer arriving soaked with a wet dryer bag is no use to your dog.

Our high-rise has a pet policy and a separate service entrance. What do you need from us?

Just tell us at booking and share the groomer details with your society office ahead of the slot. Most of the large Kharadi towers now log service visitors on an app, and a pre-approved entry means the groomer walks in rather than waiting at the desk while your slot runs down.

We moved here from abroad with our dog. Can you continue the grooming style he had?

Send a photo from the last groom when you book — it carries far more information than a style name does, since the same breed clip is cut differently in different countries. If the coat has grown out or matted since the move, we will tell you honestly whether the old style is reachable in this session or needs one intermediate cut first.

Can a dog and a cat be groomed in the same visit?

Yes, and we usually do the cat first, while the house is still quiet and before there is dog fur and dryer noise in the air. Mention both at booking so the slot is long enough and we send a groomer trained for cats — cat grooming uses different products and different handling, not the same kit on a smaller animal.

How early in the morning can you reach Kharadi?

From 9 AM reliably. Earlier is possible by arrangement, and it is the slot most of the IT crowd here asks for. The honest constraint is direction of travel — a groomer coming across from the west side hits the inbound Nagar Road peak, so early Kharadi slots are assigned to groomers already based on the eastern side of the city.
