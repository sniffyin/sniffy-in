Frequently asked questions

Is home grooming suitable for centrally located residential buildings?

Yes. Our portable equipment requires minimal space and is ideal for both modern apartments and older residential structures. We ensure zero mess and controlled operation.

Can I book recurring grooming sessions?

Yes, recurring appointments are available. Consistent scheduling helps maintain coat hygiene and reduces the chances of matting or skin issues.

Do you groom both small and large breeds?

We groom all breed sizes, from toy breeds to large dogs. Each session is customized according to coat density, size, and temperament.

How do you ensure hygiene during the session?

All tools are disinfected before and after use. Fresh towels are used for every appointment, and the working area is cleaned thoroughly once grooming is complete.

Will my pet be restrained during grooming?

We use minimal and safe restraint only when necessary for safety. Our approach focuses on calm handling rather than force.

Is grooming recommended even if my pet stays indoors?

Yes. Indoor dogs still shed, accumulate dirt, and develop nail overgrowth. Regular grooming ensures overall hygiene and health.

Parking is impossible on the lanes off FC Road and JM Road. Is that a problem?

Not for us. Our groomers travel by two-wheeler with the whole kit in a shoulder bag, which is precisely why doorstep grooming works in the older parts of central Pune at all. No four-wheeler needs to be parked and no van needs a bay.

We live in an old wada-style building with a shared bathroom and no lift. Can you still groom here?

Yes. Older central-Pune buildings are routine for us. A shared or small bathroom is workable — the groomer lays out their own mats and keeps the working area contained. The one thing worth telling us is the floor and whether there is a lift, because a large elderly dog that cannot manage three flights is better groomed on the ground floor or in a courtyard.

Do you take bookings during Ganpati week?

We do, but book early. Shivajinagar and the surrounding central lanes are among the hardest areas to reach during the festival, processions close roads at short notice, and our slot capacity there tightens for that stretch. If your dog is noise-sensitive, the more useful advice is to groom before the festival rather than during it.

Can I book just a nail trim and ear clean without the full bath?

Yes. A short maintenance visit for nails, ear cleaning and a paw-hair trim is available and priced below the packages — ask for it by name when you book. It is a sensible option for short-coated dogs between full sessions, and for senior dogs where a full bath is more strain than the coat actually needs.

Our flat faces a busy road and my dog is reactive to street noise. Does that affect grooming?

It usually just means working in the quietest room you have rather than the one nearest the road. Central Shivajinagar flats have constant traffic noise and most dogs here are habituated to it, but a reactive dog does better with the window side shut and the dryer on the lowest setting that will still dry the coat.
