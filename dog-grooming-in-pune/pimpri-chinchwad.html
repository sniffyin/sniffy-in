Frequently asked questions

Do you travel across multiple residential sectors for grooming?

Yes. Our service covers a wide residential belt, and we schedule appointments efficiently to ensure timely arrival across different neighborhoods.

Is at-home grooming safe for large breeds?

Yes. Large breeds are groomed with proper handling techniques and appropriate equipment suited to their size and coat type.

Can regular grooming improve skin health?

Absolutely. Routine brushing, bathing, and coat maintenance reduce dirt buildup and help identify early signs of irritation or infection.

How should I prepare my pet before the session?

Ensure your dog has had a short walk or bathroom break. Keeping them slightly relaxed before the session improves cooperation.

Is grooming suitable for dogs that dislike travel?

Yes. Dogs that resist car rides usually respond better when groomed at home.

Do you provide haircut styling?

Yes. Full haircut styling is available based on breed requirements and coat condition.

The air here carries industrial dust. Does that need a different approach?

It needs more thorough rinsing than a coat from central Pune does. Particulate from the industrial belt settles close to the skin and a single lather does not lift it — our groomers double-lather where the coat is visibly dull and pay particular attention to the chest and belly, which collect the most. It is also why we would not stretch a PCMC dog to twelve weeks between baths.

Do you cover Akurdi, Nigdi and Bhosari as well as Pimpri and Chinchwad?

Yes, the whole PCMC belt including Akurdi, Nigdi, Bhosari, Dapodi and the Chinchwad gaon side. It is a large area, so slot timing is worth confirming at booking — a groomer covering Nigdi in the morning and Bhosari in the afternoon needs realistic gaps between visits, and we would rather give you an honest window.

I work rotational shifts. Can I get an unusual slot?

Within our 9 AM to 9 PM window, yes, and shift workers are a normal booking here. What we cannot do is send a groomer at 6 AM or overnight. If your off-day falls midweek that actually works in your favour — midweek daytime slots in PCMC are the easiest ones to get at short notice.

We live in an older chawl-style building with no lift and a small bathroom. Is that workable?

It is, and it is a common setup across Pimpri and Chinchwad gaon. The groomer works with what the space allows and lays out their own mats. For a large dog on an upper floor without a lift, the practical question is whether the dog manages the stairs comfortably rather than whether we can work in the flat — if not, the ground floor or a courtyard is the better call.

Our building water supply comes on a schedule. How do we plan the slot around it?

Either book inside the supply window or keep three or four buckets filled beforehand. A session needs surprisingly little — the wash and rinse stages are about twenty minutes of the visit. Tell the groomer on arrival that you are on stored water and they will plan the rinse accordingly rather than assuming a running tap.
