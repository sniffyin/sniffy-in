Frequently asked questions

Do you cover independent homes and plotted developments in Katraj?

Yes. We service all property types in Katraj including society apartments and independent homes.

Can you groom multiple dogs or a dog and cat in the same visit?

Yes. Multi-pet sessions are available where scheduling allows. Please mention all animals when booking so we can plan the session time correctly.

How do you groom older or senior dogs differently?

Senior dogs need slower pacing, more breaks, non-slip surfaces, and careful attention to areas that age affects — paw pads, ear canals, and coat condition. We adapt every aspect of the session accordingly.

Is your service available during monsoon?

Yes. We operate year-round. For dogs exposed to dampness during monsoon, we use anti-fungal rinses as a preventive measure where appropriate.

What if my dog has a health condition that affects grooming?

Please mention any conditions — arthritis, skin sensitivities, anxiety — when booking. We adjust our approach and products accordingly.

How much fur ends up in the session space?

Some fur accumulation is normal during deshedding. We contain and clean it completely afterward — the working area is left clean when we're done.

Does the ghat-side humidity here affect grooming?

It affects drying time most of all. The Katraj ghat side holds moisture noticeably longer than the flatter parts of the city, and a coat that feels dry to the hand can still be damp against the skin — which is exactly how fungal patches start under a thick coat. Our groomers dry to the skin rather than to the touch, and here that stage simply takes longer.

We back onto open ground and there are strays around. What should we watch for?

Ticks above everything, and they are why we inspect before we wash rather than after. Contact with free-ranging dogs and long grass raises the odds considerably in this belt, so the check covers between the toes, both ear canals, the jawline and under the tail. If we find anything we show you where it was rather than just treating it.

We are on borewell water. Does that change the bath?

It means a longer rinse. Hard borewell water leaves mineral residue that dulls a coat and can leave the skin itchy even when the rinse felt thorough — a very common complaint across the Katraj and Ambegaon side. We rinse longer and finish with a conditioning rinse to counter it.

How old should a puppy be before its first session?

Around twelve to sixteen weeks and after the core vaccination course — confirm with your vet where your puppy currently sits. The first visit should be short and undemanding: handling paws and ears, a comb through, the dryer heard rather than used at full strength. What a puppy learns about being handled at four months shapes the next decade of sessions.

What is a sensible slot given the Sinhagad Road and Katraj commute?

Late morning through early afternoon is the most reliable window for a groomer to reach you on time. The inbound morning peak and the 6 to 8 PM return are where slot timings slip, and we would rather give you an honest window than a precise time we cannot hold.
