# How It Works — 5 step banner images (full descriptive prompts)

Each prompt below is **complete and self-contained**. Paste one at a time into the same ChatGPT chat so the style stays matched.

## The shape (why every prompt says "panorama")
The page shows each image as a very wide, short strip, about 3:1 on phones and 3.75:1 on desktop, cropped top and bottom only. The step number and heading are live text at the **bottom of one side**, so:
- Steps 1, 3, 5: text bottom-left → subject on the right.
- Steps 2, 4: text bottom-right → subject on the left.
- Keep every face inside the middle 45% of the height. No text in the images.

Save as `how-step-1.jpg` … `how-step-5.jpg` and send me the folder.

If ChatGPT returns a taller image, reply: *"Make it a much wider ultra-wide 21:9 panorama. Keep the subject smaller in the frame, in the middle band of the height, with empty space above and below."*

## Quick fixes
- **Too tall or too close?** *"Pull the camera back; make it a wider panorama with the subject smaller in the frame."*
- **Text side too busy?** *"Blur and simplify the left (or right) side so white text could sit there."*
- **Bad paws or faces?** *"Same scene, fix the anatomy."*
