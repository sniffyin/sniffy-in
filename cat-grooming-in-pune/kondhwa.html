At your doorstep

High-Rise Flats, Open Balconies, and the Risk Nobody Plans For

The one part of the session we ask you to prepare.

Kondhwa has gone vertical faster than almost anywhere in south Pune, and a large share of our sessions here happen on the ninth, twelfth, fifteenth floor. That changes one thing about the visit, and it is the only thing we genuinely need your help with.

A grooming session involves an open front door for a minute or two while the groomer brings the kit in, an unfamiliar person, and at some point a dryer. Even a placid cat can bolt at an unexpected moment, and in a high-rise the two places it heads for are the front door and the balcony. Cats do not have a reliable sense of height, and an unscreened balcony railing is not the barrier people assume it is — vets in Pune see the results of this every monsoon, when windows get left open.

So before we arrive: close the balcony door, shut any window that opens wide enough for a cat, and close off lofts and storage spaces. If your balcony is netted, tell us and we can work with it open. It takes thirty seconds and it removes the only real hazard in the entire session. The groomer will check the room before starting anyway, but you know your flat and its gaps better than we do.

Frequently asked questions

Can you groom a cat that's semi-feral or has aggression issues?

We can try, yes. We don't use heavy restraint and we won't force a session that's clearly causing distress. If your cat has a history of aggression, let us know in advance so we can plan for more time and bring appropriate supplies.

My Persian has a bad mat near the skin — can it be removed without shaving?

Often yes, if we catch it early enough. Tight skin-level mats sometimes do need to be carefully cut, but in many cases patient work with a detangler and a mat comb can remove them intact. We'll always try the non-cutting approach first.

Do you give cats a haircut or just a bath and brush?

Both options are available. Our standard session includes bath, brush, nail trim and ear clean. Haircuts — lion cuts, sanitary trims, and breed cuts — are available as add-ons. Mention it at booking.

What happens if my cat escapes or hides during the session?

It happens. We wait, give the cat space to settle, and continue when they're ready. If the cat hides and won't come out, we'll do what we can and come back for the rest at no extra charge for the return visit.

Do you cover Undri, Mohammadwadi and the NIBM Road side from Kondhwa?

Yes — Kondhwa Budruk and Khurd, NIBM Road, Undri, Mohammadwadi, Wanowrie and the Bibewadi border are all part of the same route for us. It is one of our denser coverage pockets, so short-notice slots are more often available here than in the outer suburbs. If your society is on the far side of Undri, send a location pin and we will confirm before you commit to a time.

We adopted a cat last week. Should we wait before booking a grooming session?

Wait, yes — usually two to three weeks. A newly adopted cat is still working out whether your home is safe, and adding a stranger with a dryer to that week is asking a lot. Give it time to eat normally, use the litter tray reliably and stop hiding, then book. The exception is a cat that has arrived visibly matted, flea-ridden or filthy, in which case an early session is worth the stress — tell us the situation and the groomer will keep the first visit short and focused on essentials rather than attempting a complete groom.

Does the power going out mid-session cause a problem?

Only the drying stage needs electricity, so a cut pauses things rather than ending them. Tell the groomer which sockets are on your inverter if you have one. A damp cat is genuinely not something we will walk away from — if power is out we towel-dry thoroughly, keep the room warm and wait rather than call the session finished.

Do your groomers speak Marathi or Hindi? My mother will be at home, not me.

Yes — our groomers work in Marathi, Hindi and functional English, and in Kondhwa all three come up in a normal week. Tell us at booking if an elderly family member will be handling the visit so we can make sure the groomer and the household share a language for the parts that matter.

Is it harder to get a slot around Eid and Diwali?

Noticeably, yes. Those weeks concentrate everyone's booking into the same few days across Kondhwa and NIBM, and slots go about a week ahead. For a noise-sensitive cat the more useful advice is to book before the festival rather than during it — fireworks and a stranger with a dryer on the same evening is a lot to ask.

My cat is FIV positive. Will you still groom her?

Yes. FIV is not transmissible to humans or to other species and it does not spread through grooming contact, so it changes nothing about whether we come. What it does change is care: FIV cats heal more slowly, so we are especially careful around thin skin and mat lines, and we flag any lesion or sore we find rather than working over it.

We have a balcony net for the cat. Should it stay up during the session?

Please leave it up. The riskiest moment in any home cat session is not the bath, it is the few seconds when a door opens and a startled cat bolts — and in a Kondhwa high-rise the balcony is where they head. We work in one closed room with the door shut for exactly this reason, and a netted balcony is a second layer we would rather have than not.
