Coat & skin care

Hard Water Is a Real Problem in This Belt

And it is probably why the coat never feels quite clean.

This is the complaint we hear most often in Kalewadi and across the PCMC side generally: "I bathe her myself but the fur still feels rough afterwards." Nine times out of ten the shampoo is not the issue. The water is.

Much of this belt runs on borewell or mixed supply with a high mineral load. Hard water does two things to a cat's coat. It stops shampoo lathering and rinsing properly, so a residue stays behind in the undercoat and attracts dust faster than before the bath. And the dissolved minerals themselves deposit on the hair shaft, which is what makes a freshly washed coat feel chalky and look dull rather than glossy. On a longhair cat that residue is also a mat starter, because coated hairs grip each other.

We work around it rather than complaining about it — a clarifying first wash to lift the existing buildup, a conditioner formulated to stay effective in hard water, and a much longer rinse than most people do at home, because under-rinsing is the single biggest cause of the problem repeating. If your water is particularly hard, the groomer will tell you and suggest a realistic home routine that does not make things worse between visits.

At your doorstep

Small Flats and Shared Buildings — How the Setup Actually Works

Less space than you think is needed.

A common reason people in Kalewadi put off booking is space. Flats here are compact, buildings are close together, and the mental image of "professional grooming equipment" suggests something that will not fit through the door.

In practice the groomer arrives with one bag. The washing happens in your bathroom, using your tap. The drying and combing happen on a non-slip mat laid on the floor just outside it, which needs about the footprint of a bath towel. There is no table, no tub, no cage, and nothing that has to be assembled. A 1BHK works fine; we have done plenty of sessions in less.

Compact flats also have a genuine advantage for cats. Fewer rooms means fewer places to disappear to, which sounds like a downside and is actually the opposite — a cat that cannot vanish under a bed in a far room stays in sight, stays reachable, and the session finishes faster with less chasing. The one thing worth doing before we arrive is closing the door to any loft, storage space or balcony gap, since that is where a startled cat will head first.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover Bhosari and nearby PCMC areas near Kalewadi?

Yes — Kalewadi, Bhosari, Pimple Saudagar, and surrounding pockets of north Pune are covered. Message us if you're unsure about your specific location.

Can I pay after the session is complete?

Yes, payment is collected at the end of the session — cash or UPI. You can verify you're happy with the work before paying.

My cat has fleas — should I groom or treat first?

We can use an anti-flea shampoo during the grooming session that addresses active flea presence. However, grooming alone won't eliminate a flea infestation — you'll also need to treat the home environment. We recommend talking to your vet about a comprehensive flea treatment plan, and grooming as part of that process.

Do you bring your own water, or do we need a working geyser?

We use your tap, so a working water supply is the one thing we genuinely need from you. A geyser is helpful but not essential — what matters is lukewarm water, and in Kalewadi's summers the tank supply is often warm enough on its own. If your geyser is not working, a couple of buckets of warm water kept ready is a perfectly good substitute. Cold water is the one thing we will not use; it makes cats miserable and makes drying take twice as long.

How long does my cat take to dry, and can I let her out of the room straight after?

The cat leaves the session fully dry — we do not hand back a damp cat, because a damp undercoat in this climate mats within days and can chill a smaller or older cat. Drying is done on low heat and takes anywhere from ten minutes for a shorthair to forty for a thick double coat. Once we are done she can go wherever she likes. Most cats spend the next hour indignantly re-grooming themselves, which is normal and nothing to worry about.

My white cat's fur has gone yellowish. Can a session fix that?

Often, yes, though it depends on the cause. Yellowing on a white coat in this belt is usually a mix of mineral residue from hard water and dust bonded into the hair, and a clarifying wash lifts a good part of that in one session. Two things it will not fix: staining around the eyes and mouth, which is tear and saliva pigment and needs regular wiping rather than bathing, and yellowing on an older cat whose coat oils have changed. The groomer will tell you which one you are dealing with rather than promising a result they cannot deliver.

How bad is Kalewadi Phata traffic for slot timings?

Bad enough that we plan around it. The peak morning and 6 to 8 PM stretches through the Phata are where arrival windows slip most, so we would rather book you late morning or early afternoon and actually arrive on time. Evening is possible with a wider window rather than a precise minute.

Is a compact flat actually a disadvantage for grooming a cat?

Usually the opposite. What a cat session needs is one room that closes, a tap and a plug point — and fewer places to bolt to means a cat settles considerably faster. A large open house is the harder setup, not the easier one, so a single shared bathroom is entirely workable.

Does monsoon flooding here affect appointments?

Occasionally we move a slot rather than send a groomer wading through it with electrical equipment, and there is no charge for that. What monsoon changes regardless is drying: humidity keeps a coat damp at the skin far longer, and a damp longhaired cat is how fungal patches begin, so that stage takes noticeably more time through the rains.

My parents will be at home rather than me. Do your groomers speak Marathi?

Yes — our groomers work in Marathi and Hindi as well as English, and in Kalewadi that comes up constantly. Tell us at booking if an elderly family member will be handling the visit so we can make sure the groomer and the household share a language for the instructions that actually matter.

Power cuts are common. What happens mid-session?

Only the drying stage needs electricity, so a cut pauses rather than ends the session. Tell the groomer which sockets are on the inverter if you have one. A damp cat is not something we will leave behind — if power is out we towel-dry thoroughly, keep the room warm and wait.
