What a Proper Cat Grooming Session in Kalyani Nagar Looks Like

Step by step, nothing skipped.

Here's exactly what happens during a Sniffy cat grooming session in Kalyani Nagar — from the groomer arriving at your door to leaving:

  • Arrival and setup — The groomer introduces themselves to your cat, sets up the grooming area (no mess, everything contained), and allows the cat time to investigate before starting.
  • Pre-bath brush — We brush through the coat before wetting it to remove loose fur and identify any matting that needs pre-treatment.
  • Bath — Using a cat-specific shampoo appropriate for the coat type. Temperature controlled, no sudden splashing. We support the cat throughout.
  • Conditioning and rinse — A conditioning mask is applied and left for a few minutes before thorough rinsing.
  • Drying — Low-heat blow dry with a dryer held at safe distance, or towel-dry for dryer-sensitive cats.
  • Post-bath grooming — Full brush-through, de-matting if needed, finishing comb for longhair breeds.
  • Nails, ears, eyes — Nail trim (front and back), ear cleaning with cotton and appropriate solution, eye area wipe.
  • Finish and assessment — A quick coat check and we'll let you know anything we noticed during the session.

The whole session runs 90 minutes to 2.5 hours depending on breed and coat condition.

At your doorstep

Grooming Before Travel, Boarding or a Move

Kalyani Nagar travels more than most of Pune. Plan the session around it.

A lot of households here are away regularly — work travel, long trips home, relocations in and out of the city. Where the grooming session sits relative to that matters more than people expect.

Before boarding, groom about a week ahead rather than the day before. A cat arriving at a boarding facility with a clean, mat-free, recently checked coat is easier for the staff to handle and far less likely to come back with a problem that started small — but doing it the day before stacks two stressful events on top of each other. The week's gap lets the cat reset.

Before a flight or a long road move, the same logic applies with one addition: get the claws trimmed. A cat in a carrier for eight hours with long claws will catch them in the carrier mesh, and that is a genuinely common cause of injury during transport. Do not bathe a cat immediately before travel; a coat that is even slightly damp plus air conditioning in a cargo hold or a car is a bad combination.

And after a move, wait. A cat in an unfamiliar flat is running on adrenaline for a fortnight or more, and adding a groomer to that week helps nobody. Two to three weeks after the boxes are gone is about right — by then she has claimed the new place and a stranger is merely irritating rather than alarming.

Frequently asked questions

Do you groom show cats or competition cats?

Yes. We understand show coat requirements for breeds like Persians and Maine Coons. If your cat is a show animal, mention this at booking — we take extra care with coat texture, avoid any accidental trimming, and use finishing products that enhance rather than alter the natural coat.

My cat went to a salon once and had a terrible experience. Can at-home grooming fix that?

Often yes. A single bad salon experience can create lasting associations for a cat. Home grooming in a familiar environment with a patient groomer can rebuild positive associations over time. It may take two or three sessions before the cat is fully comfortable, but progress is typically noticeable from the first visit.

Can you do two cats on the same visit?

Yes, we can groom multiple cats in sequence. Each cat gets their own session, fresh equipment, and full attention. Pricing is per cat. Let us know the number of cats and breeds when booking so we can plan the time accordingly.

Our cat has a shaved patch from a recent vet procedure. Can she still be bathed?

It depends how recent and whether the skin is closed. If there is still a suture line, a scab or any weeping, no — wait until your vet confirms it has healed, usually ten to fourteen days after a routine procedure. Once the skin is fully closed we can bathe her normally, working around the patch rather than over it and keeping shampoo off it entirely. Tell us where the patch is at booking so the groomer plans for it. The regrown fur often comes in a slightly different texture at first; that settles over a couple of months.

Do you provide a record of what was done in each session?

Yes. Every session is logged against your cat — the package, any add-ons, and the groomer's notes on coat and skin condition, ears, claws and anything they flagged. It means the next groomer arrives already knowing that your Ragdoll mats behind the left elbow or that your Persian will not tolerate the dryer near her face. If you want the notes sent to you after a session, or a summary before a vet visit, just ask and we will share it.

How do you clean equipment between cats and between homes?

Combs, brushes, clipper blades and nail clippers are washed and disinfected between every appointment, not at the end of the day. Towels and the floor mat are used once and go into a wash. Shampoo and ear cleaner are dispensed rather than dipped into. This matters more for cats than for dogs, because ringworm and ear mites transfer between cats on shared equipment very readily, and it is the single most common way a grooming visit causes a problem. If you have more than one cat in the house, we clean down between them too.

Our building requires service visitors to use the service lift. Is that an issue?

None — the groomer follows whatever your building requires and carries everything in one bag. The only thing worth flagging at booking is if the service entrance is on a different side of the complex from the main gate, which is usually where a groomer loses ten minutes inside a large Kalyani Nagar tower.

Does the humidity near the river change how long drying takes?

It lengthens it noticeably. This riverside stretch holds moisture longer than most of the city, and a longhair coat that feels dry to the hand can still be damp against the skin — which is precisely how fungal patches start. We dry down to the skin rather than to the touch, and for a Persian here that stage simply takes longer than it would elsewhere.

We are moving overseas with our cat. What grooming should happen first?

A full groom two to three days before the flight — coat combed out, no mats anywhere, nails short — so nothing needs handling on the travel day itself. Never bathe a cat on flight morning; a damp coat in a cold hold is miserable. Keep the tight scheduling for the vet health certificate, not for the grooming.

My cat has claw caps on from a previous owner. Can you remove them?

We can trim around them as they grow out, which is how they are meant to come off — they shed with the claw sheath over a few weeks. We do not apply new ones. If a cap has been left on too long and the claw underneath has overgrown into the pad, that is a vet visit rather than a grooming job, and we will tell you if that is what we find.

I am often travelling. Can building staff let the groomer in?

Someone the cat is comfortable with needs to be in the flat, and building staff usually are not that person. If a house helper who knows the cat will be there, that works and you simply need to be reachable by phone. Decisions that change the cat — a clip-down for matting — come to you directly rather than to whoever is present.
