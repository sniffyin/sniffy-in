Why Regular Grooming Matters Even for Low-Maintenance Cats

Shorthair cats need grooming too.

A lot of shorthair cat owners assume their cats are self-sufficient when it comes to coat maintenance. And to a point, they're right — shorthair cats do manage day-to-day. But nails still grow and can curl, ear wax still accumulates, and shedding without professional deshedding still means a lot of fur in your furniture. A professional session every 6–8 weeks keeps all of this managed systematically and lets us catch any emerging health issues early.

Pune seasons

The River Is Closer Than You Think, and Your Cat's Coat Knows It

Riverside humidity changes what a coat needs.

Mundwa runs along the Mula-Mutha, and the stretch of flats between the old village and the Kharadi bypass sits in noticeably damper air than, say, Kothrud does. Cat owners here often describe the same thing to us — the coat feels slightly heavy or tacky, especially through August and September, and the cat starts grooming itself more without the coat actually looking cleaner.

That is moisture sitting in the undercoat. On a shorthair it mostly means more shedding and more hairballs. On anything semi-long it means the base of the coat stays damp long enough for mats to form quietly, under the top layer, where you will not see them until they are already tight. We handle this with a full dry-out on low heat rather than a quick towel-off, and we comb from the skin outward instead of just running a brush over the top. It takes longer. It is also the only way to actually get a riverside coat dry.

Cat Breeds We Groom Most Around Mundwa

A very mixed street, in the best way.

Mundwa's cat population reflects the area itself — older village homes with generations of Indies, and newer towers with cats that arrived by breeder or adoption drive. Here is how we approach the ones we meet most often here:

  • Indie and domestic shorthair grooming in Mundwa — By far the largest group here. Usually healthy, usually not used to being handled for long. We keep the session short and efficient, prioritise nails and ears, and do a proper deshedding pass rather than a token brush.
  • Persian grooming in Mundwa — The riverside damp is hardest on this coat. We work section by section with a detangling spray, check behind the ears and under the front legs first because that is where Mundwa Persians mat, and finish with a full dry-out.
  • Siamese and Siamese-mix grooming in Mundwa — Short coats, strong opinions. They will tell you exactly how they feel about each step. We work quickly, skip anything that is not necessary, and let them supervise.
  • Bengal grooming in Mundwa — High energy, low coat maintenance. The real work is nails, which grow fast on an active cat, and a conditioning wash that keeps the rosetted coat glossy rather than dusty.
  • Senior cats of any breed — Mundwa has a lot of long-settled families with cats over ten. We groom them lying down where possible, in short blocks, and stop when they have had enough rather than pushing for a perfect finish.

If your cat is a mix of four things and looks like none of them, that is fine — we work off the coat in front of us, not the pedigree.

Frequently asked questions

My cat scratched a groomer at a salon before. Will this be an issue?

Let us know when booking. We'll note it and the groomer will come prepared with appropriate techniques for cautious cats — slow introduction, no sudden movements, and no forced restraint. Our low-restraint approach prevents most incidents before they happen.

Do I need to prepare my cat for the session in any way?

No special preparation needed. Just ensure the cat is accessible — not locked in a room where they'll feel trapped when we arrive. A light meal a couple of hours before is fine. Avoid bathing the cat yourself beforehand.

How do I know if the shampoo you're using is safe for my cat?

We only use cat-specific, pH-balanced shampoos free from essential oils, parabens, and other compounds known to be problematic for cats. We never use dog shampoo on cats. If your cat has a known skin sensitivity, mention it and we'll bring the appropriate formula.

How much does cat grooming in Mundwa cost?

Sessions start at ₹799 for a spa bath and go up to ₹1,299 for a bath with a full haircut. Nail trimming, ear cleaning, deshedding and a sanitary trim are part of the package, not extras. Optional add-ons such as a medicated bath or tick and flea care are quoted upfront before the groomer begins.

My flat is a compact 1BHK. Is there enough space to set up?

Yes. We need roughly a bathroom with a working tap and about a square metre of floor space nearby — that is genuinely it. Most Mundwa sessions happen in a standard bathroom with the drying and combing done on a mat just outside it. Smaller flats often work better for cats anyway, because there are fewer places to bolt to.

My cat stays indoors, but there are a lot of street cats around the building. Should I worry about fleas?

It is worth checking. Fleas travel indoors on shoes and clothes far more often than people expect, and ground-floor and first-floor flats near Mundwa's older lanes see this regularly. Your groomer will part the coat at the base of the tail and along the spine during the session and tell you honestly what they find. If there is an active problem, an anti-flea bath helps but will not fix the house — you will need to treat the home too.

Does the riverside humidity here affect drying?

It lengthens it. The Mundhwa riverside belt holds moisture noticeably longer than areas further inland, and a coat that feels dry to the hand can still be damp at the skin. Our groomers dry to the skin rather than to the touch here, and for a longhaired cat that stage takes the largest share of the session.

Do you cover the Keshav Nagar and Magarpatta border side?

Yes, along with the Kharadi border stretch and the older Mundhwa gaon area. It is a compact belt to work in, which usually means we can offer tighter and more reliable arrival windows here than in the more spread-out parts of the city.

Construction dust is constant around us. Does that matter for an indoor cat?

It does. Fine dust drifts indoors, settles on the surfaces the cat sleeps on, and ends up swallowed during self-grooming rather than merely sitting on the coat. While building work continues nearby, keeping to the shorter end of your cat's recommended interval is worth doing.

What happens to all the fur and nail clippings afterwards?

They leave with the groomer. Fur is collected as we go rather than swept up at the end, clippings are bagged, and the working area is cleaned before the groomer leaves. You should not be finding evidence of the session the next morning — if you do, tell us, because that is not the standard.

Can I book an evening slot?

Yes, with the last workable start around 7 PM so the coat is fully dry before the groomer leaves. We run until 9 PM, but a session starting at 8 finishes either rushed or with a damp cat. Evening slots here go early in the week, so book a few days ahead rather than the same afternoon.
