Bavdhan's Climate and Coat Care

Slightly higher altitude, drier air.

Bavdhan's elevation gives it slightly drier air than the city proper, which affects coat texture — particularly for longhair cats whose coats can become brittle with insufficient humidity. Our conditioning treatments specifically address this, and our groomers note coat hydration levels at each session so you can manage between visits if needed.

Coat & skin care

All That Green Comes Indoors With Your Cat

Bavdhan's tree cover is lovely. Your cat's coat disagrees.

Bavdhan is one of the greener parts of Pune — the hill slopes behind the societies, the tree line along the Sus and Pashan roads, gardens that actually have soil in them. It is a large part of why people move here. It also means Bavdhan cats live with a set of things that a cat in a Camp apartment simply never encounters.

Grass seeds work their way into the fur between the toes and, left alone, can burrow into the skin. Pollen settles in the coat through the flowering months and gets licked off during self-grooming, which is one of the quieter causes of seasonal itching we see. Balcony cats in this area regularly come back in with sap, tiny burrs and the occasional insect. None of it is alarming and all of it is easier to deal with early, so our Bavdhan sessions include a proper look between the toes and along the belly line rather than a glance. Owners who have never had a groomer part the fur down there are usually surprised at what turns up.

By breed

Cat Breeds We Groom Around Bavdhan

Quiet lanes, well-kept cats, some very serious coats.

Bavdhan's mix of independent houses and mid-rise societies produces an interesting spread of cats, and the drier hillside air treats each coat type differently. These are the ones we handle most often here:

  • Persian cat grooming in Bavdhan — The dry air here makes Persian coats brittle rather than greasy, which sounds better and is not. Brittle fur snaps under a comb and mats form from the broken ends. We condition heavily, comb with a wider tooth spacing than usual, and never dry-brush a Bavdhan Persian.
  • Maine Coon grooming in Bavdhan — Several in the larger houses on the hill side. Their weather-resistant coat is built for cold damp climates and does not love Pune at all; we thin the undercoat properly rather than just washing the top layer.
  • Ragdoll grooming in Bavdhan — Silky, low-grease, and prone to static in this air. A conditioning rinse and a slow low-heat dry stops the coat standing on end and matting behind the elbows.
  • Indie and domestic shorthair grooming in Bavdhan — Often with balcony or garden access, so the session is as much inspection as grooming. Paws, ears and the base of the tail first, coat second.
  • British Shorthair grooming in Bavdhan — Dense enough that owners rarely reach the skin at home. We pre-brush dry, deshed during the wash, then comb again once fully dry, which is when the real volume of loose undercoat finally comes out.

If your cat is a hillside hunter of unknown parentage, that is a Bavdhan classic and we know the type well.

Cat grooming

Starting Late: Cats Who Have Never Been Groomed

Eight years old and never seen a groomer is more common than you think.

A surprising share of our Bavdhan first-time bookings are for adult cats — five, eight, sometimes eleven years old — who have simply never had a professional session. Usually because the cat seemed fine, and because the only options used to involve a carrier and a drive.

Those first sessions need managing rather than rushing. A cat with no grooming history has no idea what any of it means: the sound of clippers, the feeling of a comb reaching skin, water that it did not choose. So the first visit is deliberately incomplete. We prioritise what is actually urgent — overgrown claws, an impacted ear, a mat that is pulling — and we stop while the cat is still tolerating it rather than pushing for a finished look. The second session, four to six weeks later, almost always goes twice as well, because the cat has learned that the person with the bag leaves and nothing bad happened. By the third, most cats are simply bored of the whole thing, which is exactly where we want them.

Frequently asked questions

Do the climate conditions in Bavdhan affect cat coat care?

Slightly, yes. The higher elevation and drier air in Bavdhan can make cat coats more prone to brittleness and static, particularly for longhair breeds. We adapt our conditioning treatment accordingly and can recommend between-session home care if it's a persistent issue.

Can you recommend a home grooming routine I can follow between sessions?

Yes — at the end of every session we're happy to suggest the right brush type and frequency for your cat's specific coat. A good home routine between professional sessions makes each visit significantly easier and keeps the coat in much better condition.

Is there a cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 4 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations with less notice may be subject to a fee. Life happens with cats — we understand — but advance notice helps us manage groomer schedules.

Do you cover the hillside societies, Sus Road and the Chandni Chowk side of Bavdhan?

Yes. Bavdhan Budruk, Bavdhan Khurd, the societies up the hill slope, the Sus Road stretch and the pockets either side of Chandni Chowk are all on our route, along with Pashan, Baner and the Kothrud border. The hill roads add a few minutes, so we tend to give a slightly wider arrival window for the higher societies — the groomer will message you when they set off.

My cat hunts lizards and insects on the balcony. Is that worth mentioning before a session?

Please do mention it. It tells the groomer to look more carefully at the mouth, chin and front paws, where scratches and small abscesses from that kind of activity tend to show up, and it is useful context if we find anything odd in the coat. It also matters for timing — if your cat has recently eaten something it caught and is off-colour, we would rather reschedule than bathe an unwell cat.

My cat is eight and has never been professionally groomed. Is it too late to start?

Not at all, but set your expectations for the first visit rather than the ideal one. An adult cat with no grooming history needs to learn the routine, so we handle the genuinely necessary things first — claws, ears, any mat that is pulling on the skin — and stop before the cat runs out of patience rather than pushing for a perfect finish. Sessions two and three go dramatically better. Most owners are surprised by how quickly a set-in-its-ways adult cat decides the whole thing is unremarkable.

What is a realistic slot given the Chandni Chowk traffic?

Mid-morning or early afternoon. The Chandni Chowk approach at peak hours is one of the least predictable stretches in the city, and we would rather book a window we can actually hold than commit to a 7 PM arrival we end up apologising for. Evening is possible with a wider window.

Our cat gets onto the terrace and there is scrub around the hillside societies. What should we check?

Ticks and grass seeds both. The hillside belt here sheds seed heads that lodge between the toes and inside the ear canal and can work under the skin if left. Our groomers check the webbing between every toe and both ears as standard — for a cat with terrace and scrub access it is one of the more valuable parts of the session.

We live in a fourth-floor flat with no lift. Does that work?

Completely. The groomer carries everything in one shoulder bag and your cat never leaves the flat — no carrier, no stairs, no car journey, which is the entire point of grooming at home. The absence of a lift affects nothing about the session.

My cat sunbathes on the terrace for hours. Does that damage the coat?

Sustained sun lightens and dries the guard hairs, and on a dark or longhaired coat it is visible by the end of summer. A conditioning treatment during the session helps the coat hold moisture. The more important point applies to white and pale-eared cats: prolonged direct sun raises real skin-cancer risk on the ear tips, and that is a conversation for your vet, not a grooming fix.

Our cat is far more settled around women. Can you send a woman groomer?

Yes — tell us at booking rather than on the day. Cats often do have a clear preference driven by voice pitch and handling style, and it is a sensible thing to plan around rather than hope for. The only caveat is availability: it may mean a different day or a wider arrival window than your first choice.
