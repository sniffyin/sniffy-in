Cat grooming

What Makes Cat Grooming Different from Dog Grooming

It's not just about the size.

Cats have fundamentally different stress responses than dogs. They're territorial rather than social, which means unfamiliar environments spike their anxiety in ways that dogs don't experience. Their skin has a different pH than dogs, requiring different shampoo chemistry. Their tolerance for restraint is lower, requiring a completely different handling approach. A groomer trained primarily on dogs can technically wash a cat — but the process will be far more stressful than it needs to be. Our groomers in Karve Nagar are trained specifically in feline grooming technique.

Long-Settled Families, Long-Lived Cats

Karve Nagar has more senior cats than almost anywhere we work.

This is not a hunch — it is what our booking sheet looks like. Karve Nagar is full of families who have lived in the same flat for fifteen or twenty years, and their cats have grown old alongside them. We regularly groom cats here who are twelve, fourteen, sixteen. That is a lovely thing, and it means the job is different.

An old cat cannot stand on a wet floor for forty minutes. Arthritic hips make the classic grooming posture — lifted, turned, held — genuinely painful. Kidney-compromised cats get cold fast. So we change the method rather than the standard: the cat stays lying down wherever possible, we work in short blocks with rests between them, we keep the water warmer and the dryer on the lowest setting, and we do the essential things first — nails, sanitary area, mats that are pulling at the skin — so that if the cat has had enough after twenty-five minutes, the important work is already done. A slightly imperfect finish on a comfortable sixteen-year-old is the right outcome. We will tell you that honestly rather than pushing on.

Coat & skin care

Nails, Scratching, and What Grooming Can Honestly Fix

Some of it. Not all of it. Here is the difference.

The single most common request we get in Karve Nagar — a neighbourhood with a lot of good furniture that has survived a lot of cats — is some version of "can you do something about the scratching?" The honest answer is partly.

Trimming the sharp hooked tip off each claw genuinely reduces the damage. A blunted claw catches far less on upholstery, and it also stops the claw curving round into the paw pad, which is a real and painful problem in older or less active cats. What trimming does not do is stop the behaviour. Scratching is how a cat marks territory and stretches its back; removing the urge is not on the table, and declawing is an amputation we will not perform or recommend under any circumstances. What actually works is the boring combination: keep the claws trimmed every four to six weeks, and put a scratching post where the cat already scratches rather than where you would prefer it. We will trim during every session and show you where the quick sits on your particular cat so you can top up between visits if you want to.

Frequently asked questions

Can I watch the entire grooming session?

Yes, you're welcome to be present and observe throughout. Some owners prefer to be there for the whole session, others check in occasionally. The groomer will tell you if your presence seems to be making the cat more anxious or helping to calm them.

What is a lion cut and is it right for my Persian?

A lion cut shaves the body coat short while leaving the head, leg tufts, and tail end as a mane. It's often recommended for severely matted Persians where de-matting is no longer possible, or for cats who are very mat-prone in Pune's humidity. It's not cosmetically ideal but it gives the cat enormous relief. We recommend it when necessary and explain the regrowth process.

How far in advance do I need to book?

Ideally 2–3 days ahead for weekday slots. Weekend bookings in Karve Nagar fill up quickly, so book 4–5 days ahead for weekends. Same-day or next-day bookings may be possible depending on groomer availability.

Do you just clip the claws, or do you file them smooth as well?

We clip and then smooth the cut edge, because a freshly clipped claw leaves a sharp square edge that can catch on fabric worse than the original hook did. We take off the tip only and stay well clear of the quick — the pink vein inside the claw. On a cat with dark claws where the quick is not visible, we take less rather than guess. We do not fit plastic claw caps and we do not declaw.

After a grooming session elsewhere, my cat stopped using her litter tray for a few days. Could grooming cause that?

It can, and it is more common than people realise. A stressful session — particularly one involving a car journey, a cage and unfamiliar animals — can leave a cat unsettled enough to break routine for a day or two, and a sanitary trim that was done too close can make squatting uncomfortable. It is one of the clearer arguments for grooming at home: the cat never leaves its own territory, so the routine has less to recover from. If it happens again after any session, mention it to us and to your vet, since it can also point to something unrelated.

Do you cover Dahanukar Colony and the Anand Nagar side?

Yes, along with the Erandwane, Warje and Shivane ends of Karve Nagar. It is a dense, established area that is easy for us to reach, so arrival windows here are usually tighter and more reliable than in the outer townships.

Water pressure in our old building is weak. Is that a problem for bathing a cat?

Not at all. The groomer works from a bucket for the wash and rinse regardless, so a thin tap just takes longer to fill. A full cat session needs about two buckets. If your supply is timed rather than merely weak, keep them ready before the slot and mention it to the groomer on arrival.

My grandchildren are usually at home. Will that disturb the session?

It can, and it is the one thing worth managing in advance. A cat under handling is already using all its patience, and children coming in and out of the room is the usual reason a settling cat stops settling. We work in one closed room — please keep it closed, particularly through the dryer and clipper stages.

Can I book just a nail trim for our elderly cat?

Yes, and for an older cat it is frequently the sensible choice. A short maintenance visit covering nails and an ear clean is available below the full session price. Overgrown claws in a senior cat can curl into the pad, which is painful and entirely preventable — keeping them short matters far more than bathing an elderly cat on a fixed schedule.

How do bookings work during Ganpati week?

Book a week ahead at minimum, and if you can, book before the festival rather than during it. Processions make the lanes off Karve Road unpredictable and we cannot hold arrival windows reliably. For a sound-sensitive cat, drumming outside plus a dryer indoors is two stressors at once.
