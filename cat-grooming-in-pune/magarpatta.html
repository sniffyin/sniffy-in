At your doorstep

Why Township Cats Do Better at Home

The gate, the lift, the car — all of it before the grooming even starts.

Getting a cat out of a Magarpatta tower and to a salon means a carrier, a lift, a walk across the podium, a gate and a drive onto the Hadapsar main road. That is a long sequence of things cats dislike, stacked before anyone has touched the coat. At-home cat grooming in Magarpatta deletes the entire sequence.

It also tends to produce a better result. A cat that arrives at a table already frightened has very little tolerance left; the same cat, in its own bedroom with the door shut, will often sit through a full de-matting session. The setting does more work than the technique.

What we do

Gate Clearance and Tower Logistics

Simpler than people assume — there is no van involved.

Send the visitor pass or app approval at booking and the groomer arrives already cleared instead of calling from the gate. Inside, we need one quiet room with a door that shuts. A bathroom is ideal, and closing that single door is normally all the containment a cat session requires.

Coat & skin care

Balcony Cats, Garden Views and the Tick Question

Indoor-outdoor cats here need a real skin check.

Magarpatta has more indoor-outdoor cats than most tower neighbourhoods — podium gardens, low balconies and the central green mean a fair number of cats get genuine outdoor time. That is good for the animal and worth being alert to for the skin: from June onwards a cat with garden access can carry ticks and fleas exactly as a dog does.

So every session includes a proper skin and ear check alongside the coat work, using cat-safe products only. This matters more for cats than dogs: permethrin-based tick products, which are ordinary for dogs, can be fatal to cats. We never improvise with dog products, whatever is to hand.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover Amanora and the Hadapsar side as well as Magarpatta City?

Yes — Magarpatta City, Amanora Park Town, Sopan Baug, Keshav Nagar, Mundhwa and the wider Hadapsar side, at the same published prices. No township premium.

Does the visitor gate pass complicate the visit?

Not if you send it at booking — the groomer arrives already cleared. There is no van to clear regardless: the whole kit comes in a bag on a two-wheeler, so the gate sees a visitor with a backpack.

My cat goes out onto the podium garden. Does she need tick treatment?

Worth checking every session, particularly from June onwards. Cats with genuine outdoor access pick up ticks and fleas much as dogs do. Important caveat: permethrin-based tick products, which are routine for dogs, can be fatal to cats — we only ever use cat-safe products, and where the problem looks veterinary rather than cosmetic we will say so.

What room do you need?

One quiet room with a door that shuts, ideally a bathroom. Closing that single door is usually all the containment a cat session needs. Tell us at booking if your cat bolts and the groomer will check the room before unpacking.

My Maine Coon takes forever to dry. How long is a session?

Longer than average — a dense longhair coat means the blow-dry stage takes most of the visit, and we do not rush it. A coat left damp at the skin in Pune humidity is where fungal trouble starts, so this is the medically useful part rather than the finish.

Do you sedate, or scruff?

Neither. Low restraint, one quiet room, breaks when the cat needs them, and we stop rather than force a session through. Sedation is a veterinary decision.

Is a lion cut appropriate for a matted Persian?

Frequently, yes. Once a coat has packed down against the skin, brushing it out means an hour of pulling; clipping is kinder and the coat regrows. We will tell you which situation you are in before starting rather than charging for a de-matting that ends in a clip.

How often should a longhaired cat be groomed?

Every 4 to 6 weeks is typical for Persians, Himalayans and Maine Coons in this climate. Combing between visits does more for the coat than a longer session does. Healthy Indian shorthairs usually need much less.

Can you do the cat and the dog in one visit?

Yes, priced as two sessions. We will usually do the cat first behind a closed door — a cat that has just heard a dryer running for an hour is considerably harder to work with.

How do I pay?

After the session, by cash or UPI, at the price quoted at booking.
