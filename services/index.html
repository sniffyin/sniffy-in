Grooming services: frequently asked questions

Are these separate services with separate prices?

They are specific uses of our three packages plus a handful of add-ons. A Spa Bath is ₹799, a Hair Cut is ₹999 and both together are ₹1,299. De-shedding and De-matting are ₹299 each, Medicated Bath, Deworming, Destress Massage and Hygiene Hair Cut are ₹299 each, Tick & Flea Care is ₹149 and Special Handling for dogs is +₹199. There is no size surcharge and no visit fee.

Which service do I need?

Start with what you notice. Hair on every surface points to de-shedding, knots and clumps to de-matting, a young animal to a first groom, an older one to senior grooming, scratching or visible ticks to tick and flea care, a pet who panics to Special Handling and a vet-diagnosed skin problem to a medicated bath. If you are unsure, say so when you book and the groomer will advise before starting.

Do you offer these for cats as well as dogs?

Yes, apart from the two dog-only extras, Special Handling and Destress Massage. Cats are groomed by groomers trained for cats, in one closed room, and we do not sedate them. The cat versions of these pages say where the approach differs.

Do I need my vet’s advice for any of them?

For a medicated bath, yes: it normally follows a diagnosis. For a puppy or kitten’s first groom, your vet’s vaccination schedule decides the date. For tick and flea prevention, your vet owns the long-term plan. For any skin, ear, eye or behaviour problem, a vet comes first, and we say so rather than treating what we have not been asked to diagnose.

How do I book one of these services?

Choose a package, add the extra you need, tell us your pet’s breed, size and any worries, and pick a slot. Groomers visit between 9 AM and 9 PM every day, you are told the groomer’s name when you book, and you pay after the session.

Do prices change by area of Pune?

No. The same prices apply across all 33 localities from Wagholi to Wakad, with no doorstep charge. The one thing that can change the plan is a coat matted so tightly that combing would hurt, in which case the groomer explains the kinder option before touching the clippers.
