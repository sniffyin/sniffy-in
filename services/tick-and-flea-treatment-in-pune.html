When tick and flea care is worth doing

Parasites are part of life in Pune, but not every pet needs treatment at every visit. We suggest the add-on only when there is a reason.

Pets with visible ticks or flea dirt

Ticks are easy to find when engorged; fleas leave tiny black specks that turn red on a damp tissue. Either means there is a problem now.

Dogs that scratch, bite or chew at themselves

Persistent itching is often fleas but can also be allergies or dry skin. The groomer checks first and tells you what they see before suggesting anything.

Pets that spend time outdoors

Gardens, parks and society compounds are where ticks wait. Dogs that walk twice a day through grass pick them up regularly.

Homes with more than one pet

Fleas travel between animals and hide in bedding. Treating one pet while the other shares the sofa means the cycle simply continues.

Pets that have just had a monsoon

Ticks and fleas multiply in humid weather. Many Pune owners notice the problem in July and August, peaking after the rains.

New adoptions and rescues

Animals with unknown history often arrive with parasites. A check at the first visit, and a vet plan afterwards, is sensible.

How the tick and flea check and treatment works

The check is part of every groom. The treatment is a separate, optional step, applied only when needed and only with your say-so.

  1. 1. Check the usual hiding places

    Between the toes, in the ears, around the neck, armpits and groin, under the tail and along the belly. Ticks prefer warm, folded skin.

  2. 2. Tell you what is found

    The groomer shows you what they have found, explains what it is and suggests whether to treat. You decide.

  3. 3. Remove visible ticks properly

    Whole-tick removal with a tick tool or fine tweezers close to the skin, steady pulling and no twisting or squeezing, so the mouthparts do not stay behind.

  4. 4. Bath with a suitable shampoo

    A normal bath removes many fleas and loose eggs. If the problem is severe, the groomer may leave the shampoo on for the contact time on the label.

  5. 5. Spot-on application, if you choose it

    The correct product for your pet’s species, weight and age, applied to the skin at the base of the neck after the coat is dry. The spot-on cost is quoted before use.

  6. 6. Aftercare advice

    What to wash, vacuum and treat at home, how long to keep your pet away from swimming and what to watch for over the next few days.

Tick & flea care: what to know before you book

Coat & skin care

Where ticks hide in Pune, and when

Not just the grass

People picture ticks as a countryside problem. In Pune they live in society gardens, empty plots, parks, the cracks between tiles in older buildings and the corners of pet beds. Brown dog ticks are the most common, and they can complete their whole life cycle indoors.

The risk climbs through the monsoon and the humid weeks after it. A tick found in September has usually been there for several days, and many households discover the problem only when a single tick becomes dozens.

The most reliable defence is a regular check. A tick the size of a sesame seed is easy to remove. One the size of a grape has been feeding for days.

  • Between the toes and under the collar
  • Inside and around the ears
  • Armpits, groin and under the tail
  • Baseboards, bedding and cracks in older flats

A weekly hands-on check, ten seconds per area, finds most problems early.

What we do

What a spot-on does, and what it does not

Treatment versus prevention

A treatment at a grooming visit kills the ticks and fleas that are on your pet right now. It is the quickest way to clear an infestation, and the one most owners want when they discover a problem.

Prevention is different. Monthly or quarterly preventives, prescribed by your vet, keep new parasites from taking hold. They are the foundation, and we are not a replacement for them. A treatment add-on does not stay on your pet for months, and the home needs cleaning too.

Fleas lay eggs in bedding, carpets and cracks, so treating the pet but not the home leads to the same problem a fortnight later. Wash bedding hot, vacuum thoroughly and bin the vacuum bag.

Think of the groomer’s add-on as the rescue and your vet’s plan as the prevention.

Cat grooming

Never use dog products on a cat

A genuinely dangerous mistake

Some tick and flea products made for dogs contain ingredients, such as permethrin, that are poisonous to cats. Applying a dog spot-on to a cat, or letting a cat lick a freshly treated dog, can cause serious illness. This is one of the most common avoidable poisonings.

We use only products labelled for cats on cats, sized by weight and age. If you have a cat and a dog, keep them apart until the dog’s treatment has dried, and ask your vet about the safest combination.

Kittens and very small puppies have minimum ages and weights for most spot-ons. If your pet is young, underweight or unwell, we will not apply anything without your vet’s confirmation.

If a product is not clearly labelled for your pet, we do not use it.

Signs that it is time to see a vet

Ticks can carry disease

Ticks can transmit illnesses such as ehrlichiosis and babesiosis in India. Symptoms can appear days to weeks after a bite and include fever, loss of appetite, lethargy, pale gums, nosebleeds, swollen joints or unusual bruising. These are emergencies, not grooming problems.

If you notice any of these, or if your pet has been bitten by many ticks, go to a vet the same day. A blood test is the only reliable diagnosis and early treatment makes a big difference.

Fleas can cause allergic skin disease and tapeworm. Constant scratching, scabs or hair loss near the base of the tail deserve a vet’s eye as well as a flea treatment.

  • Fever, lethargy or loss of appetite
  • Pale gums, nosebleeds or unusual bruising
  • Limping or swollen joints
  • Scabs, hair loss or constant scratching

When in doubt, call your vet. We will always say so, even when it means we lose the add-on.

At your doorstep

Treating the home as well as the pet

The step most people skip

Adult fleas on the pet are only a small share of the population; the rest are eggs, larvae and pupae in the environment. Wash bedding and soft toys hot every few days for a couple of weeks, vacuum floors, sofas and cracks, and empty the vacuum outside.

For ticks, check skirting, window frames and the gaps around the pet’s favourite spot. Ask your pest-control provider about pet-safe options, and keep pets out of rooms until they are dry and aired.

It takes a few weeks to break the cycle. Be consistent and do not stop treatment the moment the scratching eases.

A clean home plus a vet-planned preventive is what actually ends the problem.

Tick & flea care: frequently asked questions

How much does tick and flea treatment cost with Sniffy?

Tick & Flea Care is a ₹149 add-on. The spot-on product is priced by your pet’s size and told to you before it is applied. The tick and flea check itself is included with every package at no charge.

Will a groomer treat ticks during a regular bath?

The groomer checks for them at every visit and removes visible ticks. A normal bath washes off many fleas. If there is a real infestation, they will suggest the add-on and explain why. You decide.

Does the treatment prevent ticks for the whole month?

Spot-on products vary, and the duration depends on the product and your pet. We apply what is labelled for your pet’s weight and species. For long-term prevention, ask your vet for a monthly or quarterly preventive and follow their schedule.

Can you treat a puppy or kitten?

Only if the product is licensed for their age and weight. Most spot-ons have minimum ages, often 8 to 12 weeks. We ask you to confirm with your vet first, and we will not apply anything to a pet that is too young.

Is it safe for my cat?

Only with products labelled for cats. Some dog products contain ingredients that are toxic to cats, so we never use a dog product on a cat, and we ask you to keep them apart until it dries.

How do you remove a tick?

With a tick remover or fine tweezers close to the skin, pulling steadily and straight without twisting or squeezing, so the head does not break off. The area is cleaned afterwards. Never burn a tick or use oil.

My dog has ticks every monsoon. What can I do?

Ask your vet for a prevention plan before the rains, check your dog after every walk, keep grass short in your area, wash bedding often and book a groomer’s check every few weeks. Consistency matters more than any single treatment.

When is a tick problem an emergency?

If your pet has a fever, lethargy, loss of appetite, pale gums, bruising or nosebleeds after a tick bite, go to a vet the same day. Tick-borne illness needs a blood test and early treatment.

Do I need to treat my home too?

Yes. Most of a flea population lives in bedding, carpets and cracks, not on the pet. Wash bedding hot, vacuum often and consider a pet-safe pest-control treatment. Without this, fleas return within weeks.

Can I bathe my dog soon after a spot-on is applied?

Wait the time stated on the product label, usually 24 to 48 hours. That is why we apply the spot-on at the end of the grooming visit, once the coat is dry, rather than before the bath.
